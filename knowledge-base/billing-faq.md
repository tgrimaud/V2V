# Knowledge Base — Billing and Subscriptions

## Invoices

### How can I view my invoice?

1. Sign in to My Account at www.example-telecom.com/my-account.
2. Go to the "My Invoices" section.
3. You can download your last 12 invoices as PDF files.
4. An email with your invoice is also sent every month to the address linked to your account.

### I don't understand a charge on my invoice

Your invoice has several lines:
- **Monthly subscription**: the fixed price of your plan.
- **Options**: additional services you subscribed to (TV, Wi-Fi repeater, etc.).
- **Out-of-plan usage**: international calls, premium-rate numbers, out-of-plan texts.
- **One-off charges**: activation fees, cancellation fees, equipment purchase.

If a charge looks wrong, contact the billing team on 3900 (free from an Example Telecom line).

### My invoice is higher than usual

The most common causes:
1. **Out-of-plan usage**: check your international calls or calls to special numbers.
2. **Plan change during the month**: a pro-rata amount may apply.
3. **End of a promotion**: your discounted introductory offer may have ended.
4. **Added option**: check under "My Options" whether a recent subscription was made.

For the detail of each possible cause, see "Understanding invoice changes" below.

## Understanding invoice changes

When your invoice changes from one month to the next, the difference almost always matches one of the causes below. You can find it line by line in your invoice detail (My Account > "My Invoices").

### End of a discount or promotion

A welcome discount or a time-limited promotion has ended. The discount line that appeared in previous months disappears, and your subscription returns to its standard price. To check: compare the "Discount" or "Benefit" line between the two invoices. A new attractive offer may be available (see "My promotion has expired, can I extend it?").

### Out-of-plan overage

You used more than what is included in your plan: international calls, calls to premium-rate numbers, or out-of-plan texts. This usage appears under "Out-of-plan usage" and is added to the fixed price of your subscription. The usage detail is available in My Account.

### Option change

An option was added to or removed from your line (for example an extra TV channel, a Wi-Fi repeater, an unlimited-calls option). Adding an option increases the monthly amount; removing one lowers it. Check the "My Options" section and the "Options" line of your invoice.

### Proration (change during the month)

When a plan or option change happens during the month, billing is prorated: you pay the old plan up to the change date, then the new plan for the rest of the period. This can show two partial amounts for the same service on a single invoice. The following month, the invoice returns to a full amount.

### Taxes

The tax portion (for example VAT) is calculated on the pre-tax amount of your invoice. When your pre-tax amount changes (end of discount, added option, overage), the tax portion changes proportionally and automatically. A tax change on its own, with no other change, is unusual: contact 3900.

### One-off charge

A non-recurring charge was billed this month: activation fees, cancellation fees, equipment purchase or replacement, technician visit. This type of charge appears only once and will not recur on later invoices. It is identified under "One-off charges".

### Adjustment or correction

A correction was applied: fixing a billing error, a refund, a credit note, or carrying over an amount from a previous month. An adjustment can be positive (amount added) or negative (amount deducted). The reason is shown on the matching invoice line.

### A difference we cannot explain

If part of the difference matches none of these causes, we do not try to guess it. We connect you with a billing advisor (3900, free from an Example Telecom line) who can review your account in detail. No amount is given to you until it has been verified.

## Payment methods

### How do I change my bank details / card?

1. My Account > "My Account" > "My Payment Methods".
2. Click "Edit" next to the current direct debit.
3. Enter your new bank details (IBAN) or your new card.
4. The change takes effect from the next invoice.

### I want to pay another way

Accepted payment methods:
- Direct debit (default, recommended).
- Bank card (one-off or recurring payment).
- SEPA payment slip (attached to the paper invoice, if you chose paper billing).
- Direct-debit mandate in store.

Payment by cheque is not accepted.

### My direct debit was rejected

1. Check that your bank account has sufficient funds.
2. If your bank details changed, update them in My Account.
3. After a rejection, a new debit attempt will be made within 10 days.
4. If the second debit fails, you will receive a letter with an online payment link.
5. In case of prolonged non-payment (> 30 days), your line may be suspended.

### What plans are available?

| Comfort | 1 Gbit/s | €39.99/month | Internet + TV 80 channels + Landline |

All plans are contract-free after the first 12 months.

### How do I change my plan?

1. My Account > "My Plan" > "Change plan".
2. Choose your new plan.
3. The change takes effect within 24h for an upgrade, within 30 days for a downgrade.
4. No change fee applies during the contract period.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

1. Send a registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt to: Cancellations, Example Telecom, TSA 10101, 75999 Paris.
2. Or via My Account > "My Plan" > "Cancel".
3. The notice period is 10 days.
4. If you are still under contract, early-cancellation fees apply (remaining months × 1/4 of the monthly amount).
5. You must return the box and the TV decoder within 30 days (a pre-paid envelope will be sent to you).

## Promotions and discounts

### I have a promo code, how do I use it?

1. When subscribing online, enter the code in the "Promotional code" field before confirming.
2. If you are already a customer, some codes apply via "My Plan" > "Add a benefit".
3. Only one promo code per account is accepted.

### My promotion has expired, can I extend it?

Promotions are time-limited and are not renewed automatically.
To benefit from a new attractive offer:
1. Check the current offers at www.example-telecom.com/promotions.
2. Or call 3900 to check your eligibility for a goodwill gesture.
