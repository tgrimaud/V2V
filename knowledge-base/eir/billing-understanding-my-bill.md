# Understanding my bill

**My bundle**

- eir sells it’s products in the form of a bundle and this displays on your bill on page 3
- The bundle section will contain information on the services you have in your bundle, any promotional discount, the end date of that discount and the bundle price a month.
- A sample of what your eir bundle section looks like is shown below

**My plans and add-ons**

- The plans and add-ons section will show the any extras that you may have with your bundle and is located below your bundle section on page 3.
- Typically this will display any tv addon’s, mobile data add-on’s or if you have subscribed to Amazon Prime.
- A sample of what your plans and add-ons section looks like is shown below:

**Service charges on your bill**

- This section on your bill shows any charges for additional services like; call answering (voicemail), TV multiroom boxes or rented telephone handsets.
- A sample of what your service charges section looks like is shown below:

**My usage**

- >Your usage charges section gives a breakdown of any calls, texts and data that has been used in a specific billing period.
- This is the final section on your bill and will show a summary of usage unless you have opted into itemised billing.
- This will also include items that are covered within the allowance of your bundle.
