# Set up and general help with roaming

**Do I have roaming on my account?**

All bill pay customers are automatically set up to roam with eir mobile.

**Cannot connect to data service whilst roaming**

If you are having problems connecting to your data whilst roaming, please see some tips below that will assist you:

- Check your data allowance on your account text “ **balance roam**” to **50104.**
- Alternatively you can check through the **[eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "eir app").** Connect to a WiFi service to access the eir app.
- Switch your mobile phone off for at least 30 seconds and turn it back on.
- You can also put your mobile phone into airplane mode for at least 30 seconds, and then switch it back to normal mode.
- If the issue is still unresolved place your SIM into another handset, this will help distinguish whether the issue is with the SIM or the handset.
- If the SIM works in another handset the issue is likely to be with your device.
- If the SIM does not work in another handset, then the issue could be with your SIM card.

Have you accepted the terms and conditions that were presented in the advice of charge page? This is a text message that you will receive as you begin roaming. You will receive this message when you have reached your EU data allowance or when you begin to roam outside the EU.

**Manually selecting a network**

When you roam, your phone (unless set otherwise) will automatically search for the strongest network connection it can connect to.

However, sometimes you may need to manually select a network if you have problems making calls.

[Visit our mobile support tutorial section.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp "Visit our mobile support tutorial section.") To get the best experience of our tutorial videos, please make sure to select the handset you are currently using.

**Are you roaming outside the EU?**

**What's allowances can I use outside of the EU?**

As an eir customer, roaming charges are applicable when roaming outside of the EU. [You can check the cost of roaming here.](https://www.eir.ie/roaming "You can check the cost of roaming here.")

**Data add ons**

If you need more data, you can buy an add on just like at home. We would recommend the following add-on options:

**USA**

- 1GB roaming data Add-On
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days.

To opt in, Freetext " **Data USACAN**" to **50104**

**Rest of the World** **(Outside EU & USA)**

- 1GB roaming data Add-On
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days.

To opt in, Freetext " **DataROW**" to **50104**

**What alerts could I expect while roaming?**

**Roaming alert**

We will send you a text message on arrival to your destination, which would advise you of your roaming rates. This is referred to as “an advice of charge” message.

**Data allowance alert**

We will send you an alert when you have used up your available EU data allowance. There are 2 options available to you:

- Buy a data add-on (you can also use this when you get home).
- Accept daily usage charges - [visit eir.ie for details of charges](https://www.eir.ie/roaming/ "visit eir.ie for details of charges").

If you need more data, you can buy an add-on like at home. We would recommend one of the following add-on options:

**USA**

- 1GB roaming data Add-On
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days.

To opt in, Freetext " **Data USACAN**" to **50104**

**Rest of the World**

**(Outside EU & USA)**

- 1GB roaming data Add-On
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days.

To opt in, Freetext " **DataROW**" to **50104**

And remember you can always connect to a WiFi network if you want to save your data allowance for when you get home.
