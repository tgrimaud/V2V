# Age Friendly care

Age Friendly Care is an initiative by eir to provide a dedicated customer care and technical support service for our older customers.

Working in partnership with Age Friendly Ireland & Age Action, we designed a new care service specifically tailored to our older customers.

To speak to our dedicated Age Friendly Care team call;

[**1800 252 252**](tel:1800252252 "Age Friendly Care Team") **Monday to Friday:** **10am to 4pm**

Outside of these hours please dial our general care line [**1901.**](tel:1901 "1901")

## Help & support forms

In addition to the dedicated care line, we have developed some self service options.

> **Authorised user**\\
We would recommend that you nominate a person you trust to act on your behalf for your eir account, this person can represent your requests when you are unavailable.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=authorised-user)

**Bereavement**\\
For times where a loss impacts on an account owner and an account needs to be closed or transferred to a new owner so we can act quickly on your request.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=bereavement)

**Medical incapacitation**\\
To let us know about a change of account status due to a medical reason please use our medical incapacitation form.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=medical-incapacitation)
