# Register and log in

**How do I register for the eir app?**

- Download the eir app from the App Store or Google Play.
- Register using the temporary 085 number located on the back of your SIM pack. If you are a Broadband-only customer, use your broadband service number (which is available on your bill), or landline number.
- Already moved your number to eir? If your number is active on the eir network, use that number instead.
- We'll send a verification code to the contact number on your account. Enter the code and tap Continue.
- Enter the email address you used when signing up.
- Check your inbox and select Verify your email.
- Once verified, you'll be returned to the eir app to create a password and enter your date of birth.
- That's it! Your registration is complete and you're ready to log in to the eir app. Enjoy!

**Why can't I register using the same email address as I have for my other account?**

As you were moved to our new billing system recently, it is no longer possible to log into multiple eir accounts under one email address.

As you have more than one account which uses the same email address, you will need to register a different email address to access each of your accounts.

But if you no longer have an active account just note you can not use the old email address for 90 days after it has been terminated.

**How do I sign in?**

To sign into the eir app:

- Open the app
- Enter your registered email address
- Enter your password
- Select 'Log in'

**I have forgotten my password**

If you have forgotten your password, please follow the steps below to reset it:

1. Open the eir app and anter your email address and click 'Submit'.
2. Select 'Forgotten your password?'
3. Click I've forgotten my password; an email will be sent to your email address with details on resetting your password.

**Please note:** The link in the email will only be valid for 24 hours.

**Is there a specific iOS or android version required to use the app?**

The eir app is compatible with iOS 13 or later or Android version 4 or later. Please ensure your version is up-to-date.
