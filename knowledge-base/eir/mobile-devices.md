# Devices

**How to unlock your device**

**How do I request to unlock my handset?**

The quickest and easiest method to request to unlock a handset is to complete the Unlock a mobile form. Please click [**here**](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/) to access the self serve form.

**What do I need to complete this form?**

You will need the make and model of the handset(s) you wish to unlock, the IMEI of the handset and the mobile number currently being used in the handset.

**What is an IMEI and how do I get it?**

An IMEI is the Mobile handset equipment number. This is an identity number unique to every mobile handset and is 15 digits long.

To get the IMEI for your handset you will need to enter the code \*#06# when the handset is switched on. When this code is entered the 15 digit code will display on your screen.

**Why are you asking what mobile number is in this handset?**

We use this information to verify that the IMEI was linked to a handset used against a number on the eir network.

**How and when will you contact me?**

Once we review your unlocking code request. We will aim to contact you within 10 working days and we will respond to your request by email.

**What happens when I receive my unlocking code?**

Once we process your request we will issue your unlocking code by email.

There are two different types of response depending on the type of handset you have requested to unlock.

- For Apple Handsets

Apple handsets will be automatically unlocked by apple upon request, you will receive an email advising the handset is unlocked but no code is issued. The customer will need to sync to itunes.

- For All other handsets

eir will issue you an email with the handsets unlocking code. Upon entering this code into your handset this device will unlock and you will be able to use it on any network.

**How to find your PIN or PUK**

- Your PIN and PUK are located on the SIM pack you received when you joined eir.
- Your PIN and PUK can also be retrieved by logging in to the **[eir app](https://my.eir.ie/ "eir app") [.](https://my.eir.ie/bundles/login)**
- If you don’t have your starter pack or can’t log in to the eir app you will need to contact a member of our customer care to retrieve your PUK code.

**Lost/stolen (mobile insurance Inc)**

If your handset has been lost or stolen, we have a number of ways to help you.

You can visit one of our [71 eir retail stores](https://www.eir.ie/stores/) nationwide where a member of our Retail team will be happy to assist you with a replacement SIM and/or blacklist your mobile handset.

Alternatively, you can contact 1901 and speak to a member of our Customer Care team who will be happy to send you a replacement SIM by post and/or blacklist your mobile handset.

**\*\*Note - if your SIM is sent by post, you can activate your SIM through any of our eir Retail stores or by contacting 1901. Some customers will have the option to activate their SIM through the eir app.\*\***

If you're availing of insurance please make sure to report the incident to your local Garda station who will issue you with a crime reference number. You will also need to contact your insurance provider to report the theft within 48 hours of the incident occurring.

If you have insurance through eir the number for MobileCover is **01-2932810.**

**Mobile device & mobile broadband device repairs**

Where you purchased your mobile device will determine the repair process for you.

If you purchased the device in an eir store, please return to the same store and remember to bring the following:

- Receipt
- Mobile Device
- All packaging the Mobile device came in
- Any associated accessories
- The device manual

If you purchased your device online or over the phone, you can return it to any eir store. Please remember to bring the following with you:

- Receipt (A4 docket that came with delivery)
- Mobile Device
- The packaging the Mobile device came in
- Any associated accessories
- The device manual

Your mobile device will be repaired under warranty and without cost so long as there is no

- Water Damage
- Physical Damage
- Third Party Interference

If the warranty on your mobile device is deemed to be void you will be contacted with an estimate to repair the handset.

**Important information when returning your mobile device for repair:**

- Remove your SIM Card and store it in a safe place, as this is not needed for the repair price
- Remove your Memory Card (if any).
- Please keep a note of the IMEI number of your phone, this is the 15 digit number displayed on the back of your phone. You can also retrieve this number by dialing \* # 06 #.
- Transfer and/or delete any images, messages, downloaded materials or numbers personal to you. Your mobile device will be cleared of all personal data during the repair.
