# Scam calls

**What is a scam call?**

Scam calls are another type of 'nuisance' call and, unfortunately, cannot be blocked as they usually originate from outside Ireland.

In scam calls, the calling number most likely resembles a legitimate number which we have no control over.

Fraudsters who engage in scam calls normally choose random local or national numbers to increase the chances that the call will be answered.

**Recommendations to reduce scam calls**

If you receive unsolicited calls, emails or SMS/MMS messages, please be aware of the following:

- Never disclose any information (particularly personal or financial information).
- Delete the SMS/MMS or email message immediately.
- Never click on web links contained within such messages or download content associated with the message.
- Do not respond to calls or messages from an unknown source.
- If you have provided your personal bank account or credit card details please contact your bank immediately to advise that these details have been compromised. The financial institution will be in a position to advise regarding cancelling bank or credit cards.

**How to stop marketing calls**

We have two main ways for you to opt out of receiving marketing calls:

- You can do this manually if you are registered with [the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/bundles/login "the eir app") under the my profile section.
- Or you can call us on **1901** and one of our contact centre representatives can update this on your account for you.
- **Please note** it can take up to 28 days for your preferences to be submitted.
