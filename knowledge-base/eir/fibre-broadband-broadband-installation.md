### Broadband installation

**Changing your appointment**

To change your appointment, [please fill out the self-service form](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=change-of-appointment "please fill out the self-service form").

**Notifications about your appointment**

- Your appointment will be confirmed by email and text when you sign up.
- You will receive a reminder text the day before your appointment.
- On the day of the appointment the technician will call you before they arrive. Some work may need to be completed at the exchange before they visit your home.
- If the technician visits your home and you are not available, they will leave a calling card with details on how to reschedule your appointment.

**Technician installation**

**About your Technican Appointment**

In some instances, a technician will attend your home to activate your services.

- Your appointment will be confirmed by email and text upon signing up to your new service.
- You will also receive a reminder text from our appointment team the day before your appointment.

On the day of the appointment the technician will call you before they arrive.

- There may some work to be completed at the exchange before they attend your home.
- The technician will set up your broadband modem and ensure that your service is working at your new home.

We understand that you may not be available to meet the technician on your original appointment date. If you cannot be at home - please ensure that someone over the age of 18 is there. Alternatively you can change your appointment by [filling out the self-service form](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=change-of-appointment "filling out the self-service form").

**What time will the technician call to my home?**

Our appointments team will give you an approximate time as to when the technician will be at your home. Please see the appointments slots that our technicians.

| **Morning appointments:** | 09:00am - 13:30pm |
| **Afternoon appointments:** | 12:30pm - 16:30pm |
| **All-day appointments:** | 09:00am - 16:30pm |

**Installing the modem yourself (self-install)**

For a self-install, [please see our step-by-step guide](https://eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/#/device/eir "please see our step-by-step guide").
