# Multifactor Authentification (MFA)

**What is multifactor authentification?**

MFA, also known multifactor authentification, provides extra layers of security for your account.

We are adding further protections for your eir account by adding a verification code. This will keep your account safe even if your password is compromised.

**What should I expect when I log in to the eir app?**

When you log into the eir app for the first time, you will be asked to enter the verification code that will be sent to your phone. Once you enter it, you will also be given the option to set-up your biometric login (Face ID or fingerprint).

**I have not received a verification code?**

There may be a slight delay in receiving your code, but if it does not arrive you may find it in the spam folder within your messages.

After one minute you can request to have a new code sent out.
