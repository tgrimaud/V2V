# Fix my problem

**Forgot your password**

- Enter your email address and click next
- Click I've forgotten my password
- An email will be sent to your email address with details on resetting your password

**Forgot your username/email address**

If you have already registered for myeir, your username will be the email address you used when you first registered for my eir.

If you need to change your email address, please follow the steps below:

1. [Click here to go to the "forgot your username" page](https://my.eir.ie/forgotten-email "Click here to go to the \"forgot your username\" page").
2. Enter your landline or mobile number
3. Tick the I'm not a robot box
4. Enter your eir account number
5. Click Show me my username address

If you wish to change your registered email address, please follow the steps below:

1. Log into your my eir account
2. Click my profile on the top menu
3. Click on Update email address
4. A verification email will be sent to you - click on verify your email address
5. Your email address will update immediately
6. You will then be directed to the my eir login page

**No account found**

**New eir customer/ I have not registered for my eir**

If you have recently joined eir, you must register for my eir.

- [Click here to start the registration process](https://my.eir.ie/register "Click here to start the registration process").
- If you are a new customer, you will find your eir account number on your Welcome Email.
- If you are an existing customer, your eir account number or broadband reference number will be located on page one of your bill.

**I have already registered for my eir**

If you have already registered for myeir

- Please enter the email address you used when you first registered for myeir.

If you have forgotten this email address, [you can receive a reminder of that email address by clicking here.](https://my.eir.ie/forgot-username "you can receive a reminder of that email address by clicking here.")

If you still cannot register, get in touch with our customer service team.

**How do I find my bill?**

**Viewing your latest bill**

When you log into your my eir account you will see a summary of your latest bill on the dashboard. This will include the bill amount and payment due date.

To see a full breakdown of your bill, please follow the steps below:

- Click View Bill
- Click View bill PDF to download the full breakdown of the bill

**Viewing older bills**

If you wish to view older bills, please follow the steps below:

- Click **View Bill** on the dashboard or click **my bills** in the top right-hand corner
- Click the arrow for the drop-down beside the recent month
- This will give you a summary of the bill for that period
- Click **View Bill**
- Click **View Bill** PDF to download the full breakdown of the bill
