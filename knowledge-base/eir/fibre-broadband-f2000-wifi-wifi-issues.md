### WiFi issues

**If you're having WiFi issues**

**Ensure wireless is enabled on your device.**

Restart your modem and follow the steps below:

- First turn off the modem using the On/Off switch
- Then unplug the power source on your modem
- Wait for 30 seconds and plug it back in
- Wait a further 2 minutes for your modem to restart
- The DSL light should be solid and the internet light should be flashing - this lights will show descriptions on your modem.

Once you have restarted your modem, restart the device you are having issues with.

**What if the WiFi isn’t working?**

WiFi is disabled if the WiFi light is off. Follow these steps to turn it back on;

- Hold down the WiFi/wireless button on the modem (located on the side or rear of the modem) for 10 seconds or until the WiFi light comes back on.
- If this doesn’t work you will need to 'Forget' your wireless network on the device you are having issues with and rejoin the network again from scratch (see your devices manual for how to achieve this).

**I can't access the internet**

If you are having trouble accessing the internet, [please see our helpful tutorials to help resolve the issue](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/#/device/eir/f2000/troubleshooter/troubleshooting/i-don-t-have-internet "please see our helpful tutorials to help resolve the issue").
