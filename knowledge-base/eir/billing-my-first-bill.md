# My first bill

**How your first bill will look**

Your first bill with us will be a little different to your usual bill. There may be one-off charges relating to:

- Installation or Activation fees
- Equipment Charges (if applicable)
- Any outstanding balance from your previous plan
- Calls which are not included in your package

**When will I receive my first bill?**

Once you sign up with eir, you will automaticlly fall into the next billing cycle. Therefore, your first bill will issue within approx 10 days of your service being installed or your mobile going active. Your bills will then be issued on the same date each month.

This billing date will be visible on your first bill and also on [the eir app.](https://my.eir.ie/login "the eir app")

Your bill will be charged one month in advance from the date the bill issues.

**How to register for the eir app**

Anybody with an active eir account can register for the eir app and registering could not be easier.

When you sign up with eir you will receive an email to complete your account registration. Once you click on this you will be asked to verify your email, provide your date of birth and to set a password.

Once that is done all you need to do is go to log in when you open the app.

It really is as easy as 1/2/3!
