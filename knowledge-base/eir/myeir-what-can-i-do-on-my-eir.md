# What can I do in my eir?

**Looking to move to paperless?**

- If you are looking to move to paperless, select “my profile” from the menu on the top right hand corner in myeir.
- Scroll down to “my billing details” and opt in for “paperless billing”. Make sure to do this before your bill date in February in order to avoid any paper bill charges.

We’ll send you an email every month when your bill is ready to view on my eir. We’ll use the email address that you registered to your my eir account.

**Change password / forgotten password**

If you need to change your password, please follow the steps below:

- Enter your email address and click next
- Click I've forgotten my password
- An email will be sent to your email address with details on resetting your password.

If the 'Forgotten Password' function cannot locate your email address, [please register by clicking here.](https://my.eir.ie/register "please register by clicking here.")

If you enter your password incorrectly more than 3 times, you will automatically be directed to the reset your password page.

**Change email address**

If you have already registered for myeir, your username will be the email address you used when you first registered for my eir.

If you need to change your email address, please follow the steps below:

- Enter your email address
- Tick the I'm not a robot box
- Click Show me my email address

If you wish to change your registered email address, please follow the steps below:

- Log into your my eir account
- Click my profile on the top menu
- Click on Update email address
- An verification email will be sent to you - click on verify your email address
- Your email address will update immediately
- You will then be directed to the my eir login page

You will be asked for the number associated with your eir account - this may be your home phone number or a mobile number.

**View contract / Account information**

Managing your eir account has never been easier. If you are trying to find out about your eir services, billing information about your account or your contract end date, please see the below steps:

- Log into your my eir account
- Click my profile on the top menu

As you scroll down the page, you will be able to see

- my eir profile details
- Information on your eir products - if you click on a product, it will confirm your contract end date aswell as your Mobile Upgrade eligibility where applicable
- My Billing preferences
- My Contact preferences

**Adding an additional account number to your my eir account**

To improve your myeir experience, it is now possible to have multiple eir accounts shown under one my eir account.

To add an additional eir account to my eir, please follow the steps below:

- Log into your my eir account
- Click **my profile**
- Click **Add an account**
- Enter the account number and/or line number
- Click **add an account** to add this to your existing my eir account

**Viewing fault status on my eir**

You can view the status of an open fault in my eir. Just follow the steps below:

- On the top menu click More and then choose Check fault status
- You will see the status of any open faults you have

**Note:** It may take 24 hours after logging a fault for it to appear here.

**View your mobile PIN/PUK**

For all eir mobile customers, we have made your PIN and PUK available on my eir. To view these details, please follow the steps below:

- Click my profile on the top right corner
- Scroll down and click on the arrow beside the relevant mobile - if you have more than one mobile on the account, be sure to select the correct one
- Your PIN and PUK will now be visible

Alternatively, your PIN and PUK are located on the SIM pack you received when you joined.
