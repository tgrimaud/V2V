# Installing my broadband

**Technician install**

**About your technican appointment**

In some instances, a technician will attend your new home to activate your services.

1. Your appointment will be confirmed by email and text.
2. An email and SMS will be sent to the contact details you submitted on your Home Move form.
3. You will also receive a reminder text from our appointment team the day before your appointment

On the day of the appointment the technician will call you before they arrive.

1. There may some work to be completed at the exchange before they attend your home.
2. The technician will set up your broadband modem and ensure that your service is working at your new home.

We understand that you may not be available to meet the technician on your original appointment date. If you cannot be at home - please ensure that someone over the age of 18 is there. [Alternatively you can change your appointment date using this form.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=change-of-appointment "Alternatively you can change your appointment date using this form.")

**What time will the technician call to my home?**

Our appointments team will give you an approximate time as to when the technician will be at your home. Please see the appointments slots that are available with our technicians.

**Morning:** 09:00am - 1:30pm

**Afternoon:** 12:30pm - 4:30pm

**All-day:** 09:00am - 4:30pm
