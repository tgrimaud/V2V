# Charges

**Roaming rates by country**

**EU roaming with eir mobile**

Using your mobile while you're away costs less than you might think!

**Just like home:** Enjoy your any network calls, texts and a chunk of data from your plan just like at home.

**Same rates:** Even if you use up your domestic data, you’ll only pay the same rates as at home.

**Data add-ons:** If you need more data, you can buy an add on just like at home.

The amount of data you can use in the EU will depend on your plan. To check your roaming balance  you can log into the eir appor text "balance roam" to 50104.

| **Mobile plan** | **SMS allowance** | **Mins allowance** | **EU data allowance** |
| eir mobile Essential | Unlimited texts | 200 Irish mins | Full use of your data allowance |
| eir mobile Connect | Unlimited texts | Unlimited Irish mins | 40GB |
| eir mobile Complete | Unlimited texts | Unlimited Irish mins | 60GB |
| eir Mobile Connect - 30 Day | Unlimited texts | Unlimited Irish mins | 32GB |
| eir Mobile Complete - 30 Day | Unlimited texts | Unlimited Irish mins | 50GB |

\*From **1st of January 2026**, both Ukraine and Moldova will be included in the EU roam like home band.

There are 3 exceptions to this new rule. If you travel to Ukraine or Moldova, and call: Iceland, Liechtenstein & Norway you will be charged at ROW (rest of world) rates. These are not included in the roam like home band.

**What allowances can I use outside the EU?**

As an eir customer, roaming charges are applicable when roaming outside of the EU. [See our roaming charges](https://www.eir.ie/roaming "See our roaming charges").

**Data add-ons**

If you need more data, you can buy an add-on like at home. We would recommend one of the following add-on options:

**USA**

- 1GB roaming data add-on
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days

To opt in, Freetext "Data USACAN" to 50104.

**Rest of the World (Outside EU & USA)**

- 1GB roaming data add-on
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days

To opt in, Freetext "DataROW" to 50104.

**Monitoring data usage**

To check how much data you've used text: "balance roam" to **50104** or [log into the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login " log into the eir app").

We will also send you text alerts to help to manage your usage.

**Using your phone on a flight or at sea**

Some airlines now allow passengers to use their mobiles during their flight.

Some customers may be able to pick up some maritime service providers. We cannot guarantee coverage when you're travelling at sea.

If you are able to use these services, you will be charged the following rates:

- Making a call costs €3.99 per minute and receiving a call cost €3.99 per minute.
- Sending a text costs 39c and receiving a text is free.
- Mobile internet costs €9.99 per MB.

All pricing is quoted including VAT.

**When will roaming call and data details show on my bill?**

Roaming usage details including all calls, texts and data will show on your next bill. Sometimes it can take a little longer as you are using another operator's network while travelling.

**What alerts could I expect while roaming?**

**Roaming alert**

We will send you a text message on arrival to your destination, which would advise you of your roaming rates. This is referred to as “an advice of charge” message.

**Data Allowance Alert**

>We will send you an alert when you have used up your available EU data allowance. There are 2 options available to you:

- Buy a data add-on (you can also use this when you get home).
- Accept daily usage charges - [visit eir.ie for details of charges](https://www.eir.ie/roaming "visit eir.ie for details of charges").

If you need more data, you can buy an add-on like at home. We would recommend one of the following add-on options:

**USA**

- 1GB roaming data add-on
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days

To opt in, Freetext "Data USACAN" to 50104.

**Rest of the World (Outside EU & USA)**

- 1GB roaming data add-on
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days

To opt in, Freetext "DataROW" to 50104.

And remember you can always connect to a WiFi network if you want to save your data allowance for when you get home.
