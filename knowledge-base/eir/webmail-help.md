# Webmail help

**I can't log in**

- Clear browsing cookies, caches and history
- Double check the spelling of passwords and usernames as they are case sensitive
- Change your browsing window to incognito
- Attempt changing your password and logging back in
- Omit the @eircom.net from the email address
- When changing a password ensure that all devises that you are using to access your mail are updated with the new password, as if there is a pre-saved, incorrect password auto-populating this could cause your account to be locked out for 24hrs

**Having trouble sending or receiving emails?**

- If you are logging into your eircom.net account via a 3rd party app such as Outlook/WindowsLive Mail or Apple Mail and your ability to either send or receive has been restricted this may be because you have not signed up for the monthly subscription service.
- You will need to log in via the eir webmail portal at [**www.eir.ie/email**](https://www.eir.ie/email) to sign up and for normal service to resume.
- If you do not remember your password you may have to change it to gain access.
- When changing your password it is important that you update on all devices/apps that you use to access your account, as if there is an incorrect password auto-populating this can cause your account to be locked out for a period of time.

**Reset webmail password**

You can reset your webmail password on our **[forgotten password.](https://autoreg.eir.ie/passwordchange/)**

**Verification Code issues**

###### My verification code isn't working?

The verification code is the 6 digit code you received in the body of the SMS.

###### I Didn't Get my Verification Code?

- Double check your mobile number and click resend.
- There may be an issue with your mobile, try sending/receiving an SMS from a friend.
- Once successful you should see the below:

**External client setting (Outlook/WindowsMail/AppleMail)**

The below will allow you to update your external email client settings.

###### **Incoming Settings**

- Incoming Server: webmail.eircom.net
- Port: 995
- Authentication: Password (Normal)
- SSL/TLS (Security): SSL

###### **Outgoing (SMTP) Server Settings**

- Outgoing (SMTP) Server: mail1.eircom.net (eir broadband customers only)
- Port: 25
- Authentication: None
- SSL/TLS: None

**Can I get my emails from eir redirected to my new email address if I go to another provider?**

No, your email address will be deleted.

**Mail not saving to sent folder**

Check you have the correct mail settings.

- Click **Preferences** on the top menu bar
- Click **Mail** on the left menu bar
- Scroll down to the Composing Messages Section; make sure that Save a copy to Sent folder is ticked
- Scroll back up to the top of the page, click **Save**

**Phishing and online security**

Phishing is the attempt to obtain personal information online and is one of the most common forms of internet fraud. If you have an email account, you've probably been on the receiving end of a phishing email at one stage or another. These emails are usually very believable and if you do give personal information to phishers, they can use it for financial gain or identity theft.

Please be aware of phishing SMS (mobile phone text messages), eir will never send an SMS or email with click here or a link to enter/update payment details.

To find out more about phishing and how eir can help protect you against online threats, please see our online safety page **[here](https://www.eir.ie/online-safety/)**.
