# Roaming set up and general help

**Do I have roaming on my account?**

All prepay customers are automatically set up to roam within 5 days of joining eir mobile.

**EU roaming with eir prepay mobile**

- **Read the instructions:** We'll send you the instructions when you arrive
- **Usage of data allowance:** We'll text you when you've used up your data allowance
- **Same rates as home:** If you go over any of your allowances, don't worry, you'll only pay the same rates as you do at home.

Depending on your offer, you can use some or all of your data in the EU

**What's allowances can I use in the EU?**

You can use your any network calls & texts anywhere in the EU and they will be deducted from your allowances as they would at home.

The amount of data you can use in the EU will depend on your plan. To check your roaming data balance text **balance roam** to **50104**

| **Pre pay offer** | **SMS allowance** | **Mins allowance** | **EU data allowance** |
| €10.00 | - | - | - |
| €20.00 - Unlimited Txts Offer | Unlimited texts | - | 16.3GB |
| €20.00 - Unlimited Calls Offer | - | Unlimited Irish mins | 16.3GB |
| €30.00 | Unlimited texts | Unlimited Irish mins | 24.4GB |

**What's not included when you are roaming?**

YouTube, social networks and messaging are not unlimited while roaming - usage will be deducted from your available EU data allowance while you roam.

Free eir to eir mobile calls and texts and free calls to landlines will not be available while roaming and will be deducted from any available allowances. If you have no available allowances you will be charged at standard rates.

**Don't forget to top-up**

Dial **1741** to top-up before you go. You can also schedule top-ups to happen automatically while abroad if you think you'll need them

**Cannot connect to data service whilst roaming**

If you are having problems connecting to your data whilst roaming, please see some tips below that will assist you

- Have you accepted the terms and conditions that were presented in the advice of charge page? This is a text message that you will receive as you begin roaming.
- Switch your mobile phone off for at least 30 seconds and turn it back on. Alternatively you can put your mobile phone into airplane mode for at least 30 seconds, and then switch it back to normal mode.
- If the issue is still unresolved place your SIM into another handset, this will help distinguish whether the issue is with the SIM or the handset.
- If the SIM works in another handset the issue is likely to be with your device.
- If the SIM does not work in another handset, then the issue could be with your SIM card. If this is the case you can will need to get a new eir SIM.

When you roam, your phone (unless set otherwise) will automatically search for the strongest network connection it can connect to.

However, sometimes you may need to manually select a network if you have problems making calls.

**What's not included when you are roaming?**

YouTube, social networks and messaging are not unlimited while roaming - usage will be deducted from your available EU data allowance while you roam.

Free eir to eir mobile calls and texts and free calls to landlines will not be available while roaming and will be deducted from any available allowances. If you have no available allowances you will be charged at standard rates.

**Calling Premium Rate Numbers**

In most cases calling a premium rate number won't work while roaming (this includes calls made to Irish based companies and foreign premium providers).

If by chance you are able to connect the call, rates will vary while roaming.

**Will I be notified if I use a lot of data while roaming?**

We will send you a text message on arrival to your destination, which would advise you of your roaming rates. This is referred to as "an advice of charge" message.

- Buy a day pass.
