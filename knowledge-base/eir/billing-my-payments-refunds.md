# Refunds

**If I have overpaid how can I get a refund?**

- However, should you wish to contact us to request this, you can call us by dialling **1901**.
- Please note, that your account will need to show a credit balance for us to process a refund request

**When will I receive my mobile deposit back?**

- You may be charged a deposit when you buy a handset with us. This charge can be refunded six months after the purchase and will appear on your seventh bill
- This charge will be applied as a credit on your account. [If you wish to request this to be refunded you can by using our Refund request form](https://www.eir.ie/request-refund/ "If you wish to request this to be refunded you can by using our Refund request form")

**How to claim a refund?**

- However, should you wish to contact us to request this, you can call us by dialling **1901**.
- Please note, that your account will need to show a credit balance for us to process a refund request
- If you have left eir and given your notice to cancel and or notice to switch provider and your account is in credit, we will issue this refund automatically a month after you have cancelled. Please note that you will only receive a refund once we have received any equipment due to be returned to eir.

**I have left eir and have a credit on my final bill - how can I get this?**

- If you have left eir and given your notice to cancel and or notice to switch provider and your account is in credit, we will issue this refund automatically a month after you have cancelled. Please note that you will only receive a refund once we have received any equipment due to be returned to eir.
- You can however make this request ahead of time by completing our [completing our Refund request form.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=request-refund "completing our Refund request form.")
