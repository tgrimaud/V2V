**Resetting my modem**

You may wish to reset your modem to its factory setting. You can reset your eir modem by following the steps below:

- Locate the reset hole on your modem, which is on the rear of the device just above the power cable.
- While the modem is powered on, use a pin (or similar item) to hold down the reset button for 8-10 second until all the lights on the modem turn off. Once the lights are off, release the button.
- Allow two minutes for the device to restart and re-establish the connection to the eir network.

Alternatively, you can perform a factory reset using the software interface. To do this, please see our [guide.](https://eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/#/device/eir/fibre-box-7/topic/manage/factory-reset/2 "guide.")

**Please note:** Resetting your eir modem to factory setting will erase all customised settings you have made (network names, passwords, port forwardings etc.).
