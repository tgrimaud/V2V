### eir fibre box 7 speed

**Broadband speed checker**

Visit our [broadband speed checker](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/broadbandspeedtest) to test your speed.

**What can affect my speed?**

The broadband speed you receive is influenced by several factors:

- The type of internal network you have; while WiFi is convienient, you will generally get quicker and more consistent speeds on a wired network.
- Please ensure you are using a device capable of reaching 2G speeds. For wired speed tests you will need to use a Cat 6 standard ethernet cable to connect both the eir fibre box 7 & ONT (Optical Network Terminal) to your device.
- The ethernet cable must be connected to the 10G ethernet port at the rear of the modem. For a wireless speed test please ensure no other device is connected to the eir fibre box 7. As WiFi is a wireless technology, most materials can impact the speed, to achieve best results your device is beside the eir fibre box 7.

**Broadband speeds explained**

Broadband speed is measured in Gb/s (gigabit per second):

- Download refers to the speed with which you can receive content from the Internet. This means how quickly you can download a game or a movie.

- Upload refers to the speed with which you can send content. This could be sending an email, uploading photos to a website or filling out a web order form.
