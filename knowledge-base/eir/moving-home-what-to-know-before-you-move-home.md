# What to know before you move home

**Check eligibility at your new address**

We understand that you will want to be connected in your new home as soon as possible. We would ask that you check that your new home is eligible for eir services.

This can be done by looking up your eircode [here](https://www.eir.ie/shop/broadband).

We will make every effort to maintain your current services at your new home, but we cannot guarantee this until a member of our team has reviewed your Home Move request.

\*\*Please submit your new address details as shown on [https://www.eircode.ie/](https://www.eircode.ie/ "https://www.eircode.ie/") when completeing our [home move form](https://www.eir.ie/support/helpandsupport/?form=moving-house "Home Move Form")\*\*

**Will my contract change?**

Yes, upon moving premises you will enter a new contract.

- The contract length will depend on the services that are available in your new home.
- The duration of the new contract will be sent to the email address you provide on the Home Move form.
- **Please note** If you have Mobile handset plans the upgrade eligibility date will not change.

**Impact to activation**

Please see the table below of some scenarios that can impact your activation. The timeframes below are an industry wide standard for completion.

| Tenancy agreement - previous tenant has not cancelled their services <br>\*Timeframe varies on receipt of requested documents\* | 30 days |
| Ard key issue | 5 - 10 working days |
| Prequalification Issue - existing telephone line needs to be updated by Open eir | 5 - 10 working days |
| Exchange full | Not available |

**Please note:** The above timeframes are  an industry wide standard for completion

A member of our Home Move Team will inform you by email if your activation is affected by any of the above during your move. We would recommend that you check emails regularly once your Home Move form is submitted.

**Moving into a new build**

For any customers moving into a newly built home, please be aware that the above timeframes will not be relevant to your move.

Before you start construction please check with eir if there are any underground cables.

Then, before you apply for phone and/or broadband service:

- Ensure that all the necessary cabling (ducting) in your property is completed before you apply for phone service.
- When you have ducting completed and are ready to place your order, you will need to have your eircode which, for new builds, takes approximately one month to assign. Find out more information about how to [apply for an eircode](https://www.eircode.ie/faqs#:~:text=Eircodes%20are%20assigned%20to%20all,month%20to%20assign%20an%20Eircode).

Once you have your eircode, you can [submit your home move request here.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=moving-house "submit your home move request here.")

**Can I take my existing telephone number(s) with me when I move?**

- If you are moving within an area that is covered by your current telephone exchange, then you should be able to keep your telephone number.
- In the event that you are moving outside your current telephone exchange range, eir will have to assign you an alternative telephone number.
- Our Home Move team will manage a number change where this is available. If you are unable to keep your number, a member of our Home Move Team will contact you by email.
- **Please note**: that all customers will be provided with a temporary home telephone number on activation of their service.
