# Plans & offers

**Bill pay plans**

Take a look at our great value [bill pay deals.](https://www.eir.ie/shop/mobile?billpay=true "bill pay deals.")

**Fair usage**

For customers signing up from 22nd October 2024, the fair usage data limits for voice and 5G data do not apply on eir mobile connect plus 5G, eir mobile complete, eir mobile connect 30 day and eir mobile complete 30 day.

eir Mobile retains the right to take appropriate steps for traffic management, where for example other users are likely to be adversely affected by a Customer’s actions.

For customers that signed up before 22nd October 2024, eir is not enforcing data or voice fair usage caps on these plans either.

Text allowances where applicable in the Plans are subject to a fair usage policy of 10,000 texts.

Texts included in your mobile plan, used within Republic of Ireland and EU countries while roaming, are subject to fair usage.

The fair usage threshold in relation to data usage on the eir Mobile Essential Plan is 20GB.

Standard call, text and data rates apply to usage in excess of these limits, see [our pricing page](https://www.eir.ie/group/pricing/eir_mobile/ "our pricing page") for a full list of charges.

**Out of bundle**

You will incur charges for usage outside of your bundle allowances and these charges will be added to your next bill:

- Usage that is not inclusive in your offer
- Usage in excess of fair usage policy will be subject to the following standard rates

| **Calls** | 12c connection fee<br>39c per minute thereafter |
| **Texts** | 15c per message sent |
| **Data** | €1.99 per day up to 100MB<br>2c per MB / €20.48 per GB |
| **eir to eir calls** | 20c connection fee per call<br>(Excludes international, roaming and video calling) |
| **Voicemail** | 15c per minute |
