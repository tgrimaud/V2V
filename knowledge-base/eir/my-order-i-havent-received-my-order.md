# I have not received my order

**How to find updates on your mobile order**

- To see updates on your mobile delivery you can follow the updates we send you by text message.
- This message will contain your order reference tracking number, for updates you can check against the website of our courier partner Fastway for the exact status.
- Or alternatively you can call us on [**1901**](tel:1901 "1901") and we can check this for you.
- If after ordering there are issues with your order we will proactively make attempts to contact you to discuss your options.

**How to find updates on your broadband or TV order**

**Tracking your order status**

- To see updates on your modem or tv equipment delivery you can follow the updates we send you by text message
- This message will contain your order reference tracking number, for updates you can check against the website of our courier partner Fastway for the exact status
- Or alternatively you can call us on [**1800 303 733**](tel:1800303733 "1800 303 733") and we can check this for you.
- If after ordering there are issues with your order we will proactively make attempts to contact you to discuss your options
- However if we do not manage to get in touch with you, you can call us on [**1800 303 733**](tel:1800303733 "1800 303 733")

**What if need to change my appointment date**

Should you need to change your appointment date for an installation, please complete our **[change of appointment form](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=change-of-appointment "Change of Appointment form")** at least 24 hours before your appointment.
