### WiFi frequency

**What will I see?**

When setting up your WiFi you may see your WiFi network name appear twice, with one option ending in 2.4GHz and another ending in 5GHz.

This means there are two possible ways you can connect your device to your WiFi network.

**Which frequency should I choose?**

- Most WiFi devices will work on the 2.4GHz, such as baby monitors, cordless phones and digital TV signal senders. Sometimes these will interfere with your modem's WiFi signal strength.
- This can be managed by changing some of your WiFi settings, or by moving these devices away from your modem to reduce interference.
- 5GHz is a newer way of broadcasting a WiFi signal so there should be less interference with other WiFi devices. However the signal strength works better the closer you are to the modem.
