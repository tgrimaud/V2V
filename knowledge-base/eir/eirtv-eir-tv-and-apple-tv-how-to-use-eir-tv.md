# How to use eir TV

**General navigation**

**How to use the eirTV app**

- From the menu of your Apple TV, select eir TV
- Select Live TV
- Swipe up on the Touch surface of the remote control.
- A list of the programs which are currently broadcasting will appear.
- You can select a program here and then click on the Touch surface of the remote control.
- Press the Menu button.
- Select TV Guide and click on the Touch surface of the remote control.
- Select a TV show you would like to watch.
- To watch the TV show, select Play and click on the Touch surface of the remote control.

[Alternatively, you can take a look at our guide](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/#/device/eir/tv/topic/apple-tv/how-do-i-use-the-eir-tv-app/1 "Alternatively, you can take a look at our guide").

**Changing channels in eir TV with the eir remote**

Follow these steps to learn how to change channel and use the basic functions on your eir TV app:

- From the menu of your Apple TV, select eir TV and click on the Touch surface of the remote control.
- Select Live TV and click on the Touch surface of the remote control.
- Swipe up on the Touch surface of the remote control.
- A list with the programs which are currently broadcasting will appear.
- You can select a program here then click on the Touch surface of the remote control.
- Press the Menu button.
- Select TV Guide and click on the Touch surface of the remote control.
- Select a TV show you would like to watch.
- To watch the TV show, select Play and click on the Touch surface of the remote control.

**Managing a list of favourite channels**

**How to set up your favourite channels:**

- Select eir TV app settings.
- Click Manage Channel List.
- Select your favourite channels by clicking on the star next to them.
- Selections will appear on the left of your screen.

**Change the order they appear in:**

- Click on the up or down arrow next to the selected channel.
- To remove a channel click on the star next to the channel.

**Locate favourite channels on eirTV**

There are 2 ways to locate your favourite channels list:

**eir TV menu:**

- Go to the channels menu.
- Select my favourite channels.

**On live TV:**

- Click the up button.
- Select favourite channel.

**Parental control**

The Apple TV has built-in parental control options called Restrictions.

This allows you to restrict access to certain content and features on the Apple TV.

**eir TV PIN**

When your eir TV service was ordered, a PIN was created. This PIN can be used to add on channel packs through the eir TV app. By default, the PIN is set to 0000, but you can change this through the eir app:

- Log into [the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "the eir app").
- Click my profile on the top menu.
- Scroll down to the eir TV service and click on it.
- Find where it says TV PIN. Click Update.
- Type in a new four-digit PIN and click Save.

**Language and audio description**

Some content on eir TV will have different audio tracks available (audio descriptive, different languages and so on).

**Audio Description**

When watching a programme, swipe down on the remote's touch surface

- Select Audio.
- Select the option you want and click on the touch surface to enable.

You can also set some other audio settings here like the Dymanic range and switching to any external speakers. Not all channels or programmes will have alternate audio tracks available for them.

**Subtitles**

A lot of the content on eir TV will have subtitles that can be enabled, but please note that some channels and programmes do not have subtitles available.

- When watching a programme, swipe down on the touch surface of your remote control.
- Select subtitles.
- Select the language you wish to view the subtitles in and click on the touch surface to enable

subtitles.
- If you wish to disable subtitles, select Off and and click on the touch surface to disable subtitles.

**Adding an Apple ID to download apps**

Adding an Apple ID to download applications with eir Remote for Apple TV could not be easier. Please see the steps below:

- Click on the Apple Store icon.
- Select the Purchased Menu.
- Click on Sign in.
- Enter your email and Apple account password.
- After you have signed in, the apps you have downloaded are visible in the purchased tab.
- To download an app again select it and click on the icon.
- You can select a new app from the app store to download.
- After the apps are downloaded they will be visible in the Apple TV main menu.

**Language and audio description**

Some content on eir TV will have different audio tracks available (audio descriptive, different languages etc). If you need to change the audio please follow the steps below:

1\. Press the centre button on your remote.

2\. Select the Audio & Subtitles option.

3\. Choose between Audio & Subtitles

**Can I record with eir TV?**

Yes you can record with eir TV and you can record up to 500 programmes at the one time.

We have recording across most of our channels although recording is currently not available on RTÉ and BBC channels.

**Catch up and restart**

Depending on the programme or channel you would like to watch, the catch up or restart facility may be available.

If catch up or restart is available on a programme, you will see this by selecting the programme.
