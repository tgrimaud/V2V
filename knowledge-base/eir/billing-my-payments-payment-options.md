# Payment options

**Pay your bill by direct debit**

Direct debit is the quickest and easiest way to pay your eir bill.

You can [set up your direct debit through the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "set up your direct debit through the eir app").

Please note that a direct debit can take anywhere from 8-14 days to set up for Irish bank accounts. Any international bank can take a longer period to set up. If you have a payment due during this time, it may not be taken by direct debit and will require a manual payment. This can be done on through the **eir app** or by dialling **1901** and following the voice commands.

Once your Direct Debit is set up, we will send you confirmation by letter to notify you that your details have been processed.

For further information on the eir app, [please see our eir app support section.](https://eir.ie/helpandsupport/eir-app/eir-app-support/ "please see our eir app support section.")

**Pay your bill online**

Paying your eir bill has never been easier with the eir app.

1. Open the eir app.
2. Click on the 'Make a payment' button.
3. You can choose the amount you wish to pay and select 'Confirm'
4. Insert your card details and select 'Pay now'.

**Pay your bill in person**

You can pay your bill in person at any Payzone, Postpoint or eir Retail store nationwide.

Please allow 5 working days for your payment to reach us and reflect on the eir app. [See a list of our retail stores.](https://www.eir.ie/stores/ "Please see a list of our retail stores.")

You can also pay your bill at any Post Office. Please allow up to 5 working days for your payment to reach us and to update in your eir app.

Remember to bring along a copy of your latest bill.
