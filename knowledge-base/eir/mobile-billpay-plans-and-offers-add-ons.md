# Add ons

**Add-ons**

All eir mobile billpay customers can avail of one of our **[Data add-on offers](https://www.eir.ie/mobile/bill-pay/add-ons/ "Data add on offers").** You can choose from our list of Booster add-ons or from our recurring add-ons.

| **1\. Recurring** | Recurring add-ons will automatically renew each month unless you cancel them.<br>\*\*Add-on allowance will expire on your next billing date or when your allocated allowance has been used\*\* |
| **2\. Booster** | Booster add-ons are one off add-ons which do not recur.The will go active from the date you add them and will automatically cancel after 30 days.<br>\*\*Add-on allowance will expire in 30 days or when your allocated allowance has been used\*\* |

Please be aware that the duration indicated will not apply after you have used all of your data allocation.

**Roaming data add-on**

To get the best value on data roaming, we would recommend the following add-on options:

| **USA** | 1GB roaming data add-on<br>- Once off cost €29.99<br>- Lasts for 30 days.<br>To opt-in, Freetext **"Data USACAN"** to **50104** |
| Rest of the World<br>(Outside EU & USA) | 1GB roaming data add-on<br>- Once off cost €29.99<br>- Lasts for 30 days.<br>To opt-in, Freetext **"DataROW"** to **50104** |

**When is an add-on applied to my account and how am I charged?**

Add-ons will become active within 24 hours of you signing up.

Prorated charges are a partial charge for the time between starting the new service and your bill date.

For example if you add an add-on, on the 24th of November and your first bill issues on the 5th of December, your bill will include a part period charge from the 24th of November to the 4th of December.

**How do I cancel or change an add-on?**

The easiest way to cancel an add-on is to text the opt-out code for your add-on to **50104**.

| **Add-on** | **Opt out Code** |
| 1GB Data Booster | stop booster1 |
| 3GB Data Booster | stop booster 3 |
| 15GB Data Booster | stop booster 15 |
| 1GB Data Recurring | stop data1 |
| 3GB Data Recurring | stop data3 |
| 15GB Data Recurring | stop data15 |

**Billpay:**

The cancellation of an add-on will come into effect from your next bill date. Make sure to give us plenty of notice so we can get it cancelled well before your bill date.
