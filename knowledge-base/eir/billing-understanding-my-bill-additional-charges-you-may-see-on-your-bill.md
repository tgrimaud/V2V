# Additional charges you may see on your bill

**Mobile handset deposit**

- You may be charged a deposit when you buy a handset with us. This charge can be refunded six months after the purchase and will appear on your seventh bill.
- This charge will be applied as a credit on your account. [If you wish to request this to be refunded you can do so by using our Refund request form.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=request-refund "If you wish to request this to be refunded you can do so by using our Refund request form.")

**Unpaid direct debit fee**

- If your Direct Debit payment was returned by the bank, this will appear on your next eir bill as an Unpaid Direct Debit Fee. This currently stands at €18.45.
- This charge relates to bank charges and administration costs.
- If you have a query relating to this fee, you should contact your bank directly.
- Your bank may also charge you a fee.

**Late payment fee**

- As part of eir terms and conditions we require payment within 14 days of the bill. Should payment of the balance not be received by day 21 eir will apply a late payment fee which will appear on the next billing period.

**Early cancellation charges**

- All eir products and bundles have a contract term.
- If you cancel within this time you will be issued with an Early Cease Charge (or ECC).
- The amount you are charged depends on how long is left in your contract.
- For more information, [see our Terms and Conditions.](https://www.eir.ie/termsandconditions/ "see our Terms and Conditions.")

**Paper bill fee**

- If you have opted for paper billing, your bill will include a €5.99 charge
- This charge is the cost for printing, postage and delivery of your bills
- Should you wish to opt out of this charge, you can by amending your bills to paperless billing on the eir app.
