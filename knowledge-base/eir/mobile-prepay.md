# eir mobile prepay

**Keeping your number**

**You can keep your existing number and switch to eir**

Before you contact our customer care team, please ensure you have the following information to hand

- The account number from your current provider (if currently bill pay)
- Ensure your mobile phone is not locked to another network. If your phone is locked, the eir SIM card will not work so you must contact your current provider.
- Keep your current provider’s SIM card in your mobile phone. We need to send you a text to confirm the number you want to switch to eir.

Be sure to select the correct account type:

Billpay (Single line) – This is a billpay account where there is only one mobile number on the account.

Billpay (Multi-line) – This is a billpay account where there is more than one mobile number on the account.

Prepay (Registered) – This is a pay as you go (top up with credit) account where your current provider has your personal details.

Prepay (Unregistered) - This is a pay as you go (top up with credit) account where the provider does not have your personal details.

**How to set up a WiFi hotspot/tethering**

- Put your SIM card into your WiFi hotspot and power it on.
- On your WiFi enabled device (i.e. your mobile phone, laptop, computer or tablet) go to the wireless connection settings
- Select your hotspot from the list of available connections. The name (or SSID) of your hotspot will be written on a sticker on the back.
- Input the WiFi security key (this is an 8 digit password) and press OK.
- Enter your PIN. You will need to input this via the 'web management' page on your WiFi enabled device. To get to this page, open a web browser, type 192.168.8.1 (or 192.168.1.1 if you are using one of our older devices) into the address bar and press enter.
- Enter your PIN. You'll find this on the back of your SIM pack.

Alternatively, you can view our **[tutorial on how to use you phone as a WiFi hotspot.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/mobile/billpay/how-to-set-up-a-wifi-hotspot-tethering/ "tutorial on how to use you phone as a WiFi hotspot.")**
