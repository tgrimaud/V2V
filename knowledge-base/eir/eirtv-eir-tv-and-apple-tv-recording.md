# Recording

**Can I record with eir TV?**

Yes you can record with eir TV.

You can record up to 500 programmes at the one time.

We have recording across most of our channels although recording is currently not available on RTÉ and Virgin Media channels.

To avoid missing any of your favourite programmes that have been aired on these channels, you just need to

- Download the RTÉ player app in the App store
- Download Virgin Media player app in the App store

**How to record on eir TV**

To learn how to record TV shows on your eir TV app [please see our helpful video.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/#/device/eir/tv/topic/apple-tv/how-do-i-record-on-my-eir-tv-app/1 "please see our helpful video. ")

**Manage your recordings**

The recordings screen (recordings tab on the Online Player) lets you view and manage your recordings.

- You can see how many recordings you have left at the top of the screen. You can have up to 500!
- To watch a recording, simply tap on the image or the play button.
- More information on your recording can be found by tapping the recording's title.
- To remove or delete an episode or series recording, tap the menu button. You can choose to remove one episode, or the entire series.

**Unable to record**

- Some programmes cannot be recorded.
- When watching a live programme that you can't record, no recording options will appear.
- If you try to record an unrecordable program from the TV Guide, you'll get an error notice.
- You can record up to 500 times (the length of the recordings is irrelevant). When you hit this limit, you'll need to delete some recordings to make way for new ones.
