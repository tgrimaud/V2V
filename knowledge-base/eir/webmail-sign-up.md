# Webmail sign up

**How do I sign up?**

Currently we are not opening up webmail to new customers. If you are an existing customer you can **[log in here.](https://www.eir.ie/email/login/)**

**How much does webmail cost?**

The charge for the use of webmail is €18.99 per calendar month.

**What is the contract length?**

eir webmail is a rolling one month contract.

**How do I pay?**

- You can apply payment details on your account admin page within your webmail account.
- This is currently a recurring card payment only and can be paid with either a visa, visa debit or mastercard taken once a month.
