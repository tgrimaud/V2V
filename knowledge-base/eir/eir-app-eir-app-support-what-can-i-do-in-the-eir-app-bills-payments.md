# Bills and payments

**View latest bill.**

When you open the eir app, a summary of your latest bill will be visible on the homepage. You can view a full breakdown of the bill by selecting 'Full bill breakdown' or by downloading the PDF.

**View older bills.**

If you wish to view older bills, please follow the steps below:

1. Open the eir app and log in.
2. Select 'Bills' in the bottom menu.
3. An overview of the bill is availble by clicking 'Seebill details.
4. If you wish to view a full breakdown of the bill, please download the PDF.

**Please note**: You can view up to 12 months worth of biills.

**Make a payment.**

Paying your eir bill has never been easier with the eir app.

1. Open the eir app.
2. Click on the 'Make a payment' button.
3. You can choose the amount you wish to pay and select 'Confirm'
4. Insert your card details and select 'Pay now'.

**How do I change my payment details?**

If you wish to change your payment details, you can do so in the eir app following the steps below:

1. Open the eir app and login.
2. Navigate to the menu button in the top right-hand corner.
3. Select 'Account details'
4. Click 'Change' beside 'Payment method'.
5. The default method will be selected but you can add another method by clicking 'Add a new default method'.
6. You can choose
   - Direct Debit
   - Credit Card
   - International SEPA Mandate

**View your payments.**

You can view previous payments in the eir app by following the steps below:

- Open the eir app and log in.
- Select the 'My Payments' tab underneath your bill breakdown
- You will then be able to view your transaction history for the previous 12 months.

**Track current usage.**

If you keep track of your current usage, follow the steps below:

- Open the eir app and log in.
- Select the 'My Usage' tab underneath your bill breakdown.
- You can choose what number you want to view the usage on by using the dropdown arrow.
- Any unbilled usage will be visible under the 5 headings **e.g.** you can see what number is attached to a pending charge for a premium SMS.
