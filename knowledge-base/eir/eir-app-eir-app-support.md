## Frequently asked questions

How do I register for the eir app?

Anybody with an active eir account can register for the eir app and registering could not be easier.

When you sign up with eir you will receive an email to complete your account registration. Once you click on this you will be asked to verify your email, provide your date of birth and to set a password. Once that is done all you need to do is go to log in when you open the app.

It really is as easy as 1/2/3!

Viewing my PIN/PUK number

Your PIN and PUK are available to view on the eir app for all eir mobile customers. To view these details, please follow the steps below:

- Open the eir app and log in.
- Select 'Services' in the bottom menu.
- Under the Mobile, select 'PIN & PUK'
- Your PIN and PUK will be displayed.

View latest bill

When you open the eir app, a summary of your latest bill will be visible on the homepage. You can view a full breakdown of the bill by selecting 'Full bill breakdown' or by downloading the PDF.
