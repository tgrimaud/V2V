# Updating billing details

**Paper and paperless billing**

All new customers are opted into paperless billing unless they request paper billing.

If you would like to opt in to paper billing, there is a charge of €5.99 per bill.

The eir app stores up to 12 months of PDF bills which are available to download and print at any time.

**Changing your billing details**

Should you wish to change your billing details to update:

- Spelling
- Additional address information

You can contact our customer care team on **1901**.

**How to view your bills on the eir app**

When you open the eir app, a summary of your latest bill will be visible on the homepage. You can view a full breakdown of the bill by selecting 'Full bill breakdown' or by downloading the PDF.

If you wish to view older bills, please follow the steps below:

1. Open the eir app and log in.
2. Select 'Bills' in the bottom menu.
3. An overview of the bill is availble by clicking 'Seebill details.
4. If you wish to view a full breakdown of the bill, please download the PDF.

**Please note**: You can view up to 12 months worth of biills.
