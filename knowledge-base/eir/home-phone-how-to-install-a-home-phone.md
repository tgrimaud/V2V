# How to install a home phone

**Types of home phone service**

**Home phone (landline)**

This service is used to make telephone calls by plugging a regular telephone handset into their eir socket on the wall in their home.

This service is known as eir Talk.

**Home phone through broadband**

Home phone through broadband is a home phone service which uses your eir Fibre modem to make telephone calls.

This service works by plugging the home phone handset into the back of the modem to make and receive telephone calls.

This is known as eir Broadband Talk.

**How to install your home phone handset (landline)**

To install you home phone, follow the steps below:

- Take the grey or white telephone cable.
- Plug one end into the telephone handset.
- Plug the other end into the wall socket.
- If there is a power cable with the telephone handset plug this into the nearest power point.
- Lift the receiver and make a telephone call.

**How to install your home phone through your broadband handset**

- Take the grey or white telephone cable
- Plug one end into the telephone handset
- Plug the other end into the back of your eir Fibre modem - the socket is shown below

- If there is a power cable with the telephone handset plug this into the nearest power point.
- You can now lift the receiver and make a telephone call.
