# My bundle allowances

**Your landline plan allowances**

eir has two landline talk plans. These plans and their allowances are:

**Off Peak:**

- Unlimited local and national calls to all Irish landlines
- For use between 7pm and 7am Monday to Thursday and 7pm Friday to 7am Monday.
- Covers calls less than 60 minutes long.

**Anytime:**

- Unlimited local and national calls to all Irish landlines and Irish Mobiles
- Includes calls to international landline and international mobile calls to select destinations.
- Covers calls less than 60 minutes long.

**Your broadband allowances**

**Unlimited Broadband:**

- eir unlimited broadband has no usage caps\* or throttle limits

**30GB Usage Allowance:**

- Plan covers an allowance of 30GB.
- Usage over this allowance is subject to a charge of €2.03 per GB outside of this allowance.

**Home Mobile Broadband:**

- Home Mobile 4G and 5G broadband has a fair usage allowance of 1TB per month.

_\*Unlimited plans sold before July 2018 are subject to a fair usage allowance of 1TB. Over this allowance there is a charge of €2.00 per GB to a maximum of €100.00_

**Your mobile allowances**

**Billpay Mobile Plans:**

**eir Mobile Essential:**

- Unlimited Texts
- 200 Anytime Irish minutes
- 20GB of Data at home or in the EU.

**eir Mobile Connect Plus:**

- Unlimited Irish calls
- Unlimited Irish texts
- Unlimited Data at Home, with 40GB in the EU
- 100 international minutes and texts to select destinations

**eir Mobile Complete:**

- Unlimited Irish calls
- Unlimited Irish texts
- Unlimited Data at Home, with 60GB in the EU
- 600 international minutes and texts to select destinations

**SIM Only Plans:**

**eir Mobile Connect - 30 Day:**

- Unlimited Irish calls
- Unlimited Irish texts
- Unlimited Data at Home, with 30GB in the EU

**eir Mobile Complete - 30 Day:**

- Unlimited Irish calls
- Unlimited Irish texts
- Unlimited Data at Home, with 50GB in the EU
- 200 international minutes and texts to select destinations
