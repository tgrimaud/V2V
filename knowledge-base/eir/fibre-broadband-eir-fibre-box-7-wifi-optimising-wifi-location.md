### Optimising WiFi location

**Location and obstructions**

Try to keep the eir fibre box 7 out in the open and avoid putting it in a cupboard because the signal degrades when it passes through a dense walls or thick material for example solid concrete.

If you have a larger home your modem may not have enough power to cover the full extent of your home.

However, the **[eir WiFi 7 booster](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/broadband/eir-fibre-box-7/wifi/eir-wifi-box "eir WiFi 7 booster ")** will help boost the range of the WiFi in your home, including those hard to reach places.

**Location dos and don'ts**

**Do:**

- Ensure the eir fibre box 7 is located in the open, preferably at table height.
- Move the eir fibre box 7 away from large metal objects.
- Move any other electronic devices away from theeir fibre box 7.
- Use an eir WiFi 7 booster to extend the signal around large premises.

**Don't:**

- Place the eir fibre box 7 inside an entertainment unit, cabinet or utility room with thick walls.
- Place the eir fibre box 7 on the floor or on top of a tall object such as a bookshelf or refrigerator.
- Have a cordless phone next to or on top of the eir fibre box 7.
- Place the eir fibre box 7 at one end of the premises as it will reduce the signal quality on the opposite side of the premises.

**Interference**

Some common devices can interfere with your WiFi signal, these include (but are not limited to):

- Cordless phones
- Microwaves
- Baby monitors

Many of these devices operate on the same band of frequencies as the WiFi signal, so the closer they are to the modem the more likely they are to interfere with the WiFi.
