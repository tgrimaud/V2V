# Fix a problem

**No data service**

Make sure you complete the first steps below, to ensure its not a payment or coverage issue.

- Check your data allowance on your account through the [eir app.](https://my.eir.ie/bundles/login "eir app.")
- Ensure [mobile data](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/#/topic/mobile-internet/how-to-set-up-mobile-internet-apn-settings/device "mobile data") is turned on.
- Ensure APN settings are correct [on your device.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/#/topic/mobile-internet/how-to-set-up-mobile-internet-apn-settings/device "on your device")
- Do a location update by turning the phone off and on or by turning on flight mode.
- If the issue is still unresolved place your SIM into another handset. This will help distinguish whether the issue is with the SIM or the handset.
- If the SIM works in another handset the issue is likely to be with your device. In this case we suggest you go to your local [eir store](https://www.eir.ie/stores/) where a member of staff will access the handset and send it away for repair if necessary.
- If the SIM does not work in another handset, then the issue could be with your SIM card. If this is the case you can get a new SIM at your local [eir store](https://www.eir.ie/stores/).

**Fix coverage problem**

Mobile data speeds can be affected by external factors including but not limited to:

- The device capability
- Number of users active in the area
- Topography - valleys, mountains, trees, buildings and other obstructions.

In the case of indoor use, building insulation can negatively impact service and speed.

To help resolve this issue, carry out the below steps:

- Check the your data allowance on your account through the [eir app.](https://my.eir.ie/bundles/login "eir app.")
- Ensure [mobile data](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/) is turned on.
- Ensure APN settings are correct [on your device.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/)
- Do a location update by turning the phone off and on or by turning on flight mode.
- If the issue is still unresolved place your SIM into another handset. This will help show whether the issue is with the SIM or the handset.
- If the SIM works in another handset the issue is likely to be with your device. In this case we suggest you go to your local [eir store](https://www.eir.ie/stores/) where a member of staff will access the handset and send it away for repair if necessary.
- If the SIM does not work in another handset, then the issue could be with your SIM card. If this is the case you can get a new SIM at your local [eir store](https://www.eir.ie/stores/).

For any eir customer who is in an area that is not covered by mobile network signal, you can use eir WiFi calling as an alternative coverage option. To find out more on eir WiFi calling, [click here](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/mobile/eir-mobile-services/eir-wifi-calling/ "eir WiFi calling").

**Why can't I send an SMS?**

There are a number of reasons why you might not be able to send an SMS (text message):

- You may be prevented from sending or receiving an SMS if the handset's memory is full. Clear the inbox, outbox and sent items and power off the handset.
- Double check that you have the correct number.
- The message may have been converted from SMS to MMS. An SMS message can be converted into an MMS message on Samsung and Android devices. This can happen because:

- The SMS is going to more than one recipient.
- The message is too long (over 150 characters).
- There are pictures in the message including more than 1 emoji.

If you are a prepay customer, you may not have sufficient credit to send a MMS. To disable and prevent this: Go to the messaging app, select settings and unselect Group Messaging for MMS.
