### eir WiFi 7 booster

**What is the eir WiFi 7 booster?**

The eir WiFi 7 booster is a device which will extend your modem’s WiFi signal range.

Unlike homeplugs/WiFi boosters, it extends the WiFi signal without having to run this signal through the power cables in your home.

**Will the eir WiFi 7 booster work with my modem?**

The eir WiFi 7 booster will only work with the eir fibre box 7. If you have a modem other than the eir fibre box 7, you will need eir WiFi 6 booster.

**Connecting for the first time**

**Connecting to your eir fibre box 7:**

01. Turn on the WiFi 7 booster by plugging it in and pressing the on/off button.
02. Wait until the LED flashes red.
03. Then press the WPS buttons on your fibre box 7 for 2 second’s and on your WiFi 7 booster for 4 seconds.
04. The LED should start to flash blue.
05. The WiFi 7 booster will adopt the WiFi name of your fibre box 7.
06. Allow 5 mins for the WiFi 7 booster to pair to the fibre box 7.
07. The LED will briefly turn red, amber or green depending on the signal strength before moving to a solid white.
08. Move your WiFi 7 booster to the required location, if the LED changes to red or turns off the WiFi 7 booster is too far from the fibre box 7. Move closer to the fibre box 7 until the LED is green or amber.
09. Allow 5 mins for the WiFi 7 booster to connect to the fibre box 7 again.
10. The LED should be green for optimal performance.
11. To check the signal strength after setup press the WPS button for less then 2 seconds.
12. The LED should remain solid white, while in the default setup.

**Finding the perfect spot:**

Please try to keep the Link LED on your eir WiFi 7 booster green, it will still work if the LED is orange, but for best results ensure the LED is green.

To check this press the WPS (+) button on the WiFi 7 booster for 2 seconds, the LED will briefly change to the LINK quality green, orange or red. To improve the LINK quality simply move the eir WiFi 7 booster closer to the eir fibre box 7.

**How to check the device is working**

The LED should be solid white in standard mode.

**Basic Troubleshooting;**

**White flashing -** the WiFi 7 booster is powering up.

**Red flashing -** the WiFi 7 booster is ready to pair.

**Green flashing -** the WiFi 7 booster is too close to the fibre box 7.

**Blue flashing -** the WiFi 7 booster is in WPS mode.

**Solid blue -** WPS pairing was successful.

**Solid orange -** WPS pairing failed.

**Pink flashing -** the WiFi 7 booster is upgrading please do not switch off.

**Solid blue -** the WiFi 7 booster is in deep sleep mode.

**To check the wireless link between the WiFi 7 booster and fibre box 7**

In order to check the wireless link between the WiFi 7 booster and fibre box 7, press the WPS button for 2 seconds.

**Solid green** \- excellent connection.

**Solid orange** \- ok connection.

**Solid red** \- bad connection.
