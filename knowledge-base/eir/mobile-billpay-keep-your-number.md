# Keep your number

**Move your number to eir mobile**

**You can keep your existing number when you switch to eir.**

If you would like to move your number after you have received delivery of your eir SIM, follow the steps below:

1. Login to the eir app.
2. Select 'Keep my old number' on the My Services section of the home page.
3. Follow the steps as prompted.

   - Enter the number you want to keep (your current number) and select 'Get verification code'. **Note:** You need to keep your current SIM in your phone to receive the verification code to the number you would like to keep.
   - When you receive the code via SMS enter it into the verification code box.
   - Select your account type, date and time you would like your number to move to your new eir SIM.
   - Enter your account type and the account number from your current provider. Account number is only required for billpay accounts. This can be found on your bill or on your online account. Prepay (top-up) accounts do not require an account number to complete your move.

Be sure to select the correct account type:

Billpay (Single line) – This is a billpay account where there is only one mobile number on the account.

Billpay (Multi-line) – This is a billpay account where there is more than one mobile number on the account.

Prepay (Registered) – This is a pay as you go (top up with credit) account where your current provider has your personal details.

Prepay (Unregistered) - This is a pay as you go (top up with credit) account where the provider does not have your personal details.

**Please note:** Your number will start to move at the time and date you select. This process will take anywhere from a few minutes up to 24 hours. Leave your current SIM in your phone and wait until the signal goes. You can then place the new eir SIM into your phone and restart. Please ensure your phone is unlocked before transferring your number to the eir network.
