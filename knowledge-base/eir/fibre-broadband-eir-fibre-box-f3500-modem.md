Take a look at our [guide](https://eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/#/device/eir/fibre-box-f3500/topic/troubleshooting/my-device-won-t-turn-on "guide") if you are having trouble turning the modem on or off.

**Resetting my modem**

You may wish to reset your modem to its factory setting. You can reset your eir modem by following the steps below:

- Locate the reset hole on your modem (it is located on the rear or side of the device).
- While the modem is powered on, use a pin (or similar item) to hold down the reset button for 10-20 seconds until you see all the lights flash at once, then release the button.
- Allow two minutes for the device to restart and re-establish the connection to the eir network.

**Please note:** Resetting your eir modem to factory setting will erase all customised settings you have made (network names, passwords, port forwardings etc.).

You can also find set up guides for this (and many other tasks) for our modems [here.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/#/device/eir "Step-by-step modem guide")

**Can I use my own modem?**

**Modem:**

You can use a third party modem to connect to our services, however, we do not support these devices so we will be unable to help with the configuration of them.

The basic settings you should need to get up and running are as follows;

- Username: eircom
- Password: broadband1
- Encapsulation: PPPoE
- Multiplexing: LLC Based
- Authentication: Chap
- WAN IP Type: Dynamic
- VPI: 8
- VCI: 35

**Router:**

You can also use a third party router behind the eir modem (either as an access point or in bridge mode), however, we do not support configuring these devices.
