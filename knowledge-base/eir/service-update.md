# Storm Éowyn update

Our teams remain focused on service restoration. For the latest updates view our network outage checker.

While we are aware that some customers are still without broadband or mobile coverage, we, alongside open eir, have restored services to over 174K homes & businesses over the weekend.

We are continuing to see network damage across the country, in particular Counties Galway, Mayo, Sligo, Donegal and the northern half of the country. Our team is focused on these areas and our mobile generator deployment is ongoing for priority sites, while refuelling of these generators is also a priority for the teams today, alongside the clearance of trees blocking access to higher sites.

For the latest updates, view our [network outage checker.](https://eir.ie/network-status "network outage checker.")

Our teams have worked tirelessly battling challenging conditions and remain focused on service restoration as quickly and as safely as possible for our customers.

Our Technical Support teams are extremely busy and customers may experience excessive wait times during this period. Wait times on all other lines are not impacted.

You can use the eir app to view and pay your bill and access many other services.

Stay safe, team eir.
