# After you have submitted your home move form

**What happens next**

Once you have completed your [home move form](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=moving-house "home move form"), you will receive a confirmation email acknowledging receipt of your request.

Within 24 working hours, you will then receive your order confirmation email with your scheduled appointment and details of your new contract.

Should we require any additional information to process your home move request, we will contact you by phone.

**What do I do with my current equipment?**

When moving premises eir may request to return some equipment from your previous home. If this is necessary, we will send you an email advising of the next steps.

Please see the below table:

| **Equipment type** | **What to do** |
| Broadband modem | Return within 30 days |
| TV set-top box | Return within 30 days |

For further information on how to return your equipment, [please click here.](https://eirreturns.blocworx.com/ "please click here.")
