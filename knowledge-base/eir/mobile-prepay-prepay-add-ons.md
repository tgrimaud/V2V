# Prepay add-ons

**Prepay add-ons**

Add-ons are applied to your account, at your request. You will  be charged at a discounted rate to avail of:

- Extra minutes
- Extra texts
- Extra data

There are two types of add-ons:

**Recurring add-ons:** Will automatically renew every month (28 days) provided there is a sufficient balance on the account. Customers must Opt-out of these add-ons or they will continue to auto renew at the end of each cycle. Customers can Opt-out by texting the opt-out code reference to 50104.

**Note:** That if an reoccurring add-on is removed within the 28 day cycle then you will lose any allocation of data from said add-on

**Booster add-ons:** are one off add-ons that do not renew.

How it works

1\. [Top up](https://eir.ie/mobile/top-up/ "Top up")

2\. Choose your add-on

3\. Free text the keyword to 50104

**How do I opt in to an add-on?**

Ensure you have enough credit on your account to activate your add-on. Then choose your add-on and freetext the relevant keyword to **50104**.

**How do I cancel or change an add-on?**

The easiest way to cancel an add-on is to text the opt out code for your add-on to 50104.

Please note you will not keep the allocation of your previous add-on if you cancel in the middle of your 28 days.

**How do I check the balance of an add-on?**

To check the balance of an add-on freetext "data balance' to **50104** or [log into the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "log into the eir app").

**Can I use an add-on while roaming in the EU?**

Add-ons can be used subject to fair usage. [Please check our add-ons page for information in relation to each add-on.](https://eir.ie/mobile/prepay/add-ons/ "Please check our add-ons page for information in relation to each add-on.")
