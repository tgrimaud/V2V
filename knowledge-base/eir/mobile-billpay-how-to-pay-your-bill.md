# How to pay your eir bill

**Pay your bill by direct debit**

Direct debit is the quickest and easiest way to pay your eir bill.

You can set this up through [the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "the eir app ") if you have not already done so or you can call our care team on **1901**.

Your direct debit can take anywhere from 8-14 days to set up for Irish bank accounts. Any international bank can take a longer period to set up.

Once we set up your direct debit we will send you confirmation your details by letter to notify you that your details have been processed.

**Pay your bill online**

The quickest way to pay your bill is by using [the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "the eir app") service.

Most payments will reflect in the balance straight away, but some may take up to 48 hours to show.

**Pay your bill over the phone**

If you wish to pay your bill over the phone, please follow the steps below:

- Call **1901.**
- Have your account number and the card you are paying with to hand.
- Follow the voice commands.
- That's it!

**Pay your bill in person**

Visit a retail store that displays the Postpoint or Payzone symbol. Please remember to bring your bill with you.

Please allow 48 hours for your payment to reach us and to show on your eir app account. Our list of [retail stores](https://www.eir.ie/stores/ "retail stores") **.**

**Pay your bill by cheque**

Send us a cheque along with the payment slip from your bill made payable to eir and post to:

PO Box 43,

Kilrush,

Co. Clare.

If you pay by cheque, please note it will take 5 working days from the date eir receives your cheque to process the payment and show in your my eir account.
