# Fix a problem

**Check your handset and its connection**

**Check your handset**

To check that the problem isn’t with your handset, please try a different handset.

**Check your connection**

- Remove all other connections (e.g. extension cables, phones, tv boxes, game consoles etc.).
- Reconnect the phone directly to the main socket.
- If you can hear a dial tone and make a call then the issue is not with your phone line and could be with your handset.
- Ensure the telephone lead is securely plugged directly into the telephone socket and the handset is connected to the phone itself or, if you're using a cordless phone, to the base of the phone,
- To minimise interference on the line, your base should be placed away from large metal items and electronic equipment, such as fridge-freezers, TVs etc.
- Try to have the distance between the base and handset hidden by as few walls as possible.

**Is it a new phone line?**

If you were advised that your landline would be in service within 24 hours and it hasn't automatically connected, we may need to send a technician to investigate.

Please call us on **1901** from a mobile to notify us as your line may be showing active on our system.

**Have you just had broadband installed?**

If you cannot hear a tone, there may be a fault with the line or you may have been supplied with a faulty splitter. Please contact our technical support department on **1901**.

**After checking my connection I still have no dial tone**

**There is still no dial tone**

If you have completed the cable and handset checks and your home phone line is still not working please contact our technical support team on **1901** and they will log a fault for you.

**Will I be charged for repairs?**

- There is no call out charge for the maintenance of all wiring up to and including the main telephone socket.
- In all other cases a call out charge will apply.
- Our working hours are Monday to Friday 8:30 am to 5pm.
