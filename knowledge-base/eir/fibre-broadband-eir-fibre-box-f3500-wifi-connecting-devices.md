### Connecting devices

**Using an ethernet cable**

Connecting to your eir modem using an ethernet cable is the most reliable way to get an internet connection to your device as very little speed is lost over the cable connection.

To connect your device using an ethernet cable, do the following:

- Connect one end of the ethernet cable to the ethernet port on your device (usually on the side or rear of the device).
- Connect the other end of the ethernet cable to one of the four ethernet connections on the rear of the eir modem.
- Allow 30 seconds or so for the connection to be established.

**Using the WiFi connection**

Connecting to your eir modem using the WiFi connection allows you greater freedom for the use and location of your device.

However, as WiFi is a radio signal, it will generally result in lower speeds depending on your proximity to the modem and any obstructions in the path of the signal.
