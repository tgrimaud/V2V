# Roaming charges

**Roaming rates by country**

**EU roaming with eir mobile**

All pre pay customers are automatically set up to roam automatically within 5 days of joining eir mobile.

**EU roaming with eir pre pay mobile**

- **Read the instructions:** We'll send you the instructions when you arrive.
- **Usage of data allowance:** We'll text you when you've used up your data allowance.
- **Same rates as home:** If you go over any of your allowances, don't worry, you'll only pay the same rates as you do at home.

Depending on your offer, you can use some or all of your data in the EU.

**What's allowances can I use in the EU?**

You can use your any network calls & texts anywhere in the EU and they will be deducted from your allowances as they would at home.

The amount of data you can use in the EU will depend on your plan. To check your roaming data balance txt **balance** **roam to 50104**.

| **Pre pay offer** | **SMS allowance** | **Mins allowance** | **EU data allowance** |
| €10.00 | - | - | - |
| €20.00 - Unlimited Txts Offer | Unlimited txts | - | 13.1GB |
| €20.00 - Unlimited Calls Offer | - | Unlimited Irish mins | 13.1GB |
| €30.00 | Unlimited txts | Unlimited Irish mins | 19.6GB |

\*From **1st of January 2026**, both Ukraine and Moldova will be included in the EU roam like home band.

There are 3 exceptions to this new rule. If you travel to Ukraine or Moldova, and call: Iceland, Liechtenstein & Norway you will be charged at ROW (rest of world) rates. These are not included in the roam like home band.

**Outside the EU**

**What's allowances can I use outside of the EU?**

As an eir customer, roaming charges are applicable when roaming outside of the EU. Check the [cost of roaming](https://www.eir.ie/roaming/#/ "cost of roaming")

To get the best value on data roaming, we would recommend one of the following Add on options:

**Data add-ons**

If you need more data, you can buy an add-on like at home. We would recommend one of the following add-on options:

**USA**

- 1GB roaming data Add-On
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days.

To opt in, Freetext "Data USACAN" to 50104

**Rest of the World (Outside EU & USA)**

- 1GB roaming data Add-On
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days.

To opt in, Freetext "DataROW" to 50104

[Click here for more details on our data add-on](https://www.eir.ie/roaming/ "Click here for more details on our data add-on").

**Calling Premium Rate Numbers**

In most cases calling a premium rate number won't work while roaming (this includes calls made to Irish based companies and foreign premium providers).

If by chance you are able to connect the call, rates will vary while roaming.

**How do I Contact Customer care while Abroad?**

The number to call while abroad is : **+353 1 766 2037**

**Monitoring data usage**

To check how much data you've used text "balance roam" to **50104** or [log into the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "log into the eir app").

We will also send you text alerts to help to manage your usage.

**Using your phone on a flight or at sea**

Some airlines now allow passengers to use their mobiles during their flight.

Some customers may be able to pick up some maritime service providers. We cannot guarantee coverage when you're travelling at sea.

If you are able to use these services, you will be charged the following rates:

- Making a call costs €2.33 per minute and receiving a call cost €2.23 per minute.
- Sending a text costs 39c and receiving a text is free.
- Mobile internet costs €4.99 per MB.

All pricing is quoted including VAT.

**What allowances can I use in the EU?**

Using your mobile while you're away costs less than you might think!

- **Just like home:** Enjoy your any network calls, texts and a chunk of data from your plan just like at home.
- **Same rates:** Even if you use up your domestic data, you’ll only pay the same rates as at home.
- **Data add-ons:** If you need more data, you can buy an add on just like at home.

The amount of data you can use in the EU will depend on your plan. To check your roaming balance [you can log into the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "you can log into the eir app") or text " **balance** **roam" to 50104**.

| **Mobile Plan** | **SMS Allowance** | **Mins Allowance** | **EU Data Allowance** |
| **eir Mobile Essential** | Unlimited texts | 200 Irish mins | 20GB |
| **eir Mobile Connect Plus** | Unlimited texts | Unlimited Irish mins | 40GB |
| **eir Mobile Complete** | Unlimited texts | Unlimited Irish mins | 60GB |

**Will you send me data text alerts while roaming?**

**Roaming alert**

We will send you a text message on arrival to your destination, which would advise you of your roaming rates. This is referred to as "an advice of charge" message.

**Data allowance alert**

We will send you an alert when you have used up your available EU data allowance. There are 2 options available to you:

- Buy a data add-on (you can also use this when you get home).
- Buy a day pass.

If you need more data, you can buy an add-on like at home. We would recommend one of the following add-on options:

**USA**

- 1GB roaming data Add-On
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days.

To opt in, Freetext "Data USACAN" to 50104

**Rest of the World (Outside EU & USA)**

- 1GB roaming data Add-On
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days.

To opt in, Freetext "DataROW" to 50104

And remember you can always connect to a WiFi network if you want to save your data allowance for when you get home.
