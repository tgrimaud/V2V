# Add-ons

**Add-ons**

Add-ons are applied to your account at your request. You will be charged at a discounted rate to avail of:

- Extra minutes
- Extra texts
- Extra data

There are two types of add-ons:

1. **Recurring:** Recurring add-ons automatically renew every month provided there is a sufficient balance on your account. Recurring add-ons will automatically renew unless you cancel them.
2. **Booster:** Booster add-ons are one off add-ons which do not recur.

**Roaming data add-on**

To get the best value on data roaming, we would recommend the following add-on options:

**USA**

- 1GB roaming data add-on
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days.

To opt in, Freetext " **Data USACAN**" to **50104**

**Rest of the World (Outside EU & USA)**

- 1GB roaming data add-on
- Once off cost €29.99
- Lasts for 30 days.

To opt in, Freetext " **DataROW**" to **50104**

**When is an add-on applied to my account and how am I charged?**

Add-ons will become active within 24 hours of you signing up.

The cost of the add on will be deducted from your credit balance.

**How do I cancel or change an add-on?**

The easiest way to cancel an add-on is to text the opt-out code for your add-on to **50104**.

| **Add-on** | **Opt out code** |
| **1GB Data Booster** | stop booster1 |
| **3GB Data Booster** | stop booster3 |
| **15GB Data Booster** | stop booster15 |
| **1GB Data Recurring** | stop data1 |
| **3GB Data Recurring** | stop data3 |
| **15GB Data Recurring** | stop data15 |
