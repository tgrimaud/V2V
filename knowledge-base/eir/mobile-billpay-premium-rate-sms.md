# Premium rate SMS

**What is premium rate SMS?**

Premium rate services are provided by private companies for a higher price than ordinary SMS/calls.

Examples of such services are: TV competitions and subscription services.

Premium SMS's consist of 5 digit numbers and begin with the number 5 e.g. **5XXXX**

**Opt out of premium rate SMS**

Customers can now opt out of being able to send premium SMS.

Mobile Customers can opt out by:

- Texting: **PRSMSBAR** to **50104**

The above bar applies to shortcodes in the ranges **53XXX** and **57XXX** and applies to messages sent and received.

It can take up to 24 hours for this change to occur.
