# Our home phone plans

**Off peak landline plan**

eir's Off Peak Landline plan is our entry level landline talk plan.

This plan covers Irish local and national calls from 7pm to 7am Monday to Thursday and over the weekend from 7pm Friday to 7am Monday morning.

Calls outside these times and over 60 minutes in duration will be charged at standard rates.

Please note this plan’s allowance does not include Bank Holidays or international calling.

**Anytime landline plan**

eir’s Anytime Landline plan is our premium landline talk plan.

This plan covers local, national and Irish mobile calls anytime day or night. This plan also covers select international landlines and mobiles.

Calls over 60 minutes in duration will be charged at standard rates.
