# Help with eir TV

**Picture issues**

If you think your problem might be with the display settings, [please visit the Apple support website.](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/HT200197 "please visit the Apple support website.")

**Connection issues**

If you think it's a connectivity issue, [please visit the Apple support website.](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/HT204400 "please visit the Apple support website. ")

**eir TV sound issues**

If you are having problems with the sound on your Apple TV, here are some things you can do to fix it. There are several reasons you might be experiencing problems with your sound:

###### **Volume and Muting**

- First of all, make sure your television is not on mute. Look for a symbol of a speaker with a diagonal line across it. It should be on the screen as well as your remote control.
- Click it to mute / unmute

- Hold the volume up button on the TV remote for several seconds. The television's volume can also be controlled using the Apple TV remote.

If you require further help with the issue, [please visit the Apple Support website.](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/tv/atvba773c3c9/tvos "please visit the Apple Support website. ")

**I can't see a particular programme / I'm getting an error**

Here are the possible reasons for this:

- You may not be subscribed to this channel, in which case the programmes are greyed out in the programme guide.
- If you receive an error when attempting to watch a channel, try opening another channel and go back.
- If this does not resolve the problem, close the eir TV app and re-open the eir TV app to attempt to watch the channel again. A guide on how to close apps on your AppleTV set top box is shown in the video below.

**Cables and connections**

If the Apple TV and the television volume are working but there is still no sound, checking the cables is the next step

- Locate the HDMI cable and unplug it at both ends, then plug it back in
- Try swapping the ends of the HDMI. In other words, plug the end that was in the television into the Apple TV box and vice versa
- If you have a different HDMI cable, try using that one. Also try using a different HDMI port on your TV
- Try a different device in each of the HDMI ports.
