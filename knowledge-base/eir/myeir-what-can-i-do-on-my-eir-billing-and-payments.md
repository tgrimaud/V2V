# Billing and payments

**View my bill**

**Viewing your latest bill**

When you log into your my eir account you will see a summary of your latest bill on the dashboard. This will include the bill amount and payment due date.

To see a full breakdown of your bill, please follow the steps below:

1. [Log into your my eir account](https://my.eir.ie/login "Log into your my eir account")
2. Click View Bill
3. Click View bill PDF to download the full breakdown of the bill

**Viewing older bills;**

If you wish to view older bills, please follow the steps below:

1. [Log into your my eir account](https://my.eir.ie/login "Log into your my eir account")
2. Click View Bill on the dashboard or  click my bills in the top right-hand corner
3. Click the arrow for the drop-down beside the recent month
4. This will give you a summary of the bill for that period
5. Click View Bill
6. Click View bill PDF to download the full breakdown of the bill

**Make a payment**

Paying your eir bill has never been easier with my eir.

How to make a Payment with Credit/Debit card

1. [Log into your my eir account](https://my.eir.ie/login "Log into your my eir account")
2. Click on pay now on your dashboard
3. Enter payment amount and card details
4. Follow the on screen prompts

We also offer many more convenient ways to pay your bill.

1: Over the phone:

Contact Customer Care on 1901

- Have your account number
- Have your payment card details to hand
- Follow the voice commands

2: Pay in person

Visit a retail store

- Bring your bill with you

3: Pay by cheque

Send us a cheque along with the payment slip from your bill made payable to eir and post to:

PO Box 43, Kilrush Co. Clare.

**How do I change my payment details?**

You can change your Direct Debit details online in my eir. Please note that this may take up to 10 days to complete the update to your account to reflect the change.

Set up or change your Direct Debit

1. **[Log into your my eir account](https://my.eir.ie/login "Log into your my eir account")**
2. Click on My Profile
3. Scroll down to My billing details
4. Click Edit
5. Enter your bank account/credit card details

[If you wish to add/amend international payment details fill out our international direct debit form](https://www.eir.ie/opencms/export/sites/default/.content/pdf/sepa-international-direct-debit.pdf "If you wish to add/amend international payment details fill out our international direct debit form"). If we need to send you this form we will advise of the return address on that form.

Please note we cannot accept these requests via email. If you are cancelling your direct debit you will need to contact the eir customer care team on **1901** in order for us to process this request.
