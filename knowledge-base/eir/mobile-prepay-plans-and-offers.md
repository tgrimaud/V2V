# Plans and offers

**Mobile plans**

Take a look at our [prepay plans](https://www.eir.ie/mobile/prepay/activate/).

**Activating prepay offers**

Our prepay offers activate on a 4 week (28 day) or 2 week (14 day) cycle.

- Offers will only activate if the full cost of activation in your account.
- The credit is used to pay for your data, calls and texts so when you top up your credit is deducted from your account.
- Plans will activate upon receipt of an activation text.

We will send you a text message at the end of your offer cycle advising you to top up so  that there is no disruption to activation from one cycle to the next.

**How to activate a Prepay plan**

- Select the Simplicity offer that's right for you
- Text the [opt-in code](https://www.eir.ie/mobile/prepay/activate/ "opt-in code") to **50104** and top up by the correct amount.

Your offer should be active in minutes, just wait for two texts to confirm your top up has been successful.

**Fair usage policy (FUP)**

For customers signing up from 22nd October 2024, the fair usage data limits do not apply to 5G data. eir Mobile retains the right to take appropriate steps for traffic management, where for example other users are likely to be adversely affected by a customer’s actions.

For customers that signed up before 22nd October 2024, eir is not enforcing data fair usage caps on prepay Simplicity plans for 5G data.

Minutes and texts on Simplicity offers are subject to a Fair Usage Policy (FUP) of:

3,000 minutes for calls and 3,000 texts.

For more see [Simplicity Plan terms and conditions.](https://www.eir.ie/opencms/export/sites/default/.content/pdf/terms/meteor/simplicity_offer.pdf "Simplicity Terms and Conditions")

**Out of bundle usage**

You will incur charges for usage outside of your bundle allowances. These charges will be deducted from your credit balance.

- Usage in the absence of an active offer
- Usage that is not inclusive in your offer
- Usage in excess of Fair Usage Policy will be subject to the following standard rates

**Calls:** 12c connection fee, then 39c per minute subject to per minute billing

**Texts:** 15c per message sent

**Data:** €1.99 per day up to 100MB and 2c per MB / €20.48 per GB

**Calls to eir Customer Care:** 20c connection fee per call

**Voicemail:** 15c per minute
