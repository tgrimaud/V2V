# What is my eir?

**What is my eir?**

my eir is a self service portal that allows you to view and manage your account without the need to get in touch with us.

**What can you see on my eir?**

**Self Service**

- Pay a bill
- Set up or amend your direct debit or recurring card payment details
- Switch on/off paperless billing
- Download PDF bills
- Check your payment due date

**Manage your account**

- View balance and the last 12 month's bills
- Track your usage and out of bundle charges for your current billing period
- Compare bills
- View PIN/PUK for mobile and TV
- View your UAN
- View your payment history

**Mobile**

**Bill pay**

- Check mobile services enabled on your account
- Check your eligibility upgrade
- Upgrade eligibility check

**Pre pay**

- Check your remaining credit balance
- Check your remaining minutes, texts, or data balance

**Profile**

- Change your email address or password
- Manage directory enquiries and contact preferences
- View up to 50 eir accounts under the one on my eir account
- Check the status of a landline or broadband fault
- Manage your eir WiFi guest and host settings

**How do I register for my eir?**

**Who can register for my eir?**

Anybody with an active eir account can register for my eir!

**What do I need to register?**

Registering for my eir could not be easier, you will need some of the following account details depending on the services you have with us:

- Home Phone Service customers or customers with multiple services including a landline will need their 8 digit account number as well as their landline phone number.
- Standalone broadband customers should use their broadband reference number in place of the phone number. This number will start with 888.
- Prepay Mobile customers will need their eir mobile number.
- Standalone Billpay Mobile customers will need their 8 digit account number as well as their eir mobile number.

If you are a new customer, you will find your eir account number on your Welcome Email. If you are an existing customer, your eir account number or broadband reference number will be located on page one of your bill.
