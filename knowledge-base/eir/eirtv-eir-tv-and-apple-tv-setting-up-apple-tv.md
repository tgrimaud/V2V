**Connecting the Apple TV**

There are only two cables you will need to connect to get up and running, the power cable and a HDMI cable.

1. Connect the HDMI cable into the rear of the Apple TV and connect the other end to a free HDMI port on your TV set
2. Connect the power cable to the back of the Apple TV box and plug it into a working power outlet
3. Once the Apple TV box has power you should see a blinking white light on the front
4. Make sure your TV set is powered on and on the correct input source

Once everything is ready to go, you should see the setup screen on your TV.

**Initial set-up**

**1\. Plug it in and turn on your TV**

Plug your Apple TV into power and connect it to your TV with an HDMI cable.

Then turn on your TV and select the HDMI input that your Apple TV is connected to.

If you don't want to connect to Wi-Fi during set-up, you can connect your Apple TV to your router with an Ethernet cable.

**2\. Choose your language, and turn on Siri**

To choose an option, click the Touch surface. If you choose the wrong language, press the Menu button to return to the previous screen.

**3\. Continue setup with your iOS device or set up manually**

To automatically add your Apple ID and Wi-Fi settings to your Apple TV, choose Set-Up with Device.

If you don't want to set-up your Apple TV with your iOS device, choose Set-Up Manually. Then follow the onscreen steps on your Apple TV to connect to your home Wi-Fi network and sign in with your Apple ID.

**4\. Choose settings**

Turn on One Home Screen to keep the Home screen and apps the same across every Apple TV.

**5\. Get apps and start streaming**

When set-up is complete, you'll see the Home screen. From here, you can watch your eirTV app.

**Accessing the eir TV app**

Once you have set up your Apple TV, the eir TV app should be automatically installed

- The eirTV app icon should now appear on the Apple TV Home screen.
- Using the trackpad on the eir remote, highlight the eir TV icon and click the trackpad to open.
- The eir TV app should sign in automatically without any need for a username or password.
- You can move the eir TV app to the Top Shelf on the Apple TV Home screen:

a. Highlight the app

b. Click and hold the touchpad on the remote until the App shakes on screen

c. Drag the app to wherever you want to place it on the screen

d. Click on the touchpad one more time to confirm it's position

**Note:** To quickly get to the Apple TV Home screen, press and hold the Menu button on the Apple TV Remote.

**Do I need to have an iTunes account to use the eir TV app on AppleTV?**

No, the eir TV app will download and be placed on your Apple TV. Authentication will take place seamlessly.

**Adding an Apple ID to download Apps**

Adding an Apple ID to download applications with eir Remote for Apple TV could not be easier. Please see the steps below:

- Click on the Apple Store icon
- Select the Purchased Menu
- Click on Sign in
- Enter your email and Apple account password
- After you have signed in, the apps uou have downloaded ate visible in the purchased tab
- To download an app again select it and click on the icon
- You can select a new app from the app store to download
- After the apps are downloaded they will be visible in the Apple TV main menu
