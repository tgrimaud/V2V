# eir TV mobile app

**How do I download the eir TV app?**

It couldn't be easier to download the app, just follow the steps below:

- You must first be registered on [the eir app.](https://my.eir.ie/login "the eir app. ")
- You can download it from the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tv.accedo.via.android.sports&hl=en_IE) or the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ie/app/eir-tv/id1126856440) on iOS.
- Once you have downloaded the eir TV app, you can log in using the username and password from your [eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "eir app") account.

**How to set / edit favourite channels**

**How to set up your favourite channels:**

- Select eir TV app settings
- Click Manage Channel List
- Select your favourite channels by clicking on the star next to them
- Selections will appear on the left of your screen

**Change the order they appear in:**

- Click on the up or down arrow next to the selected channel
- To remove a channel click on the star next to the channel

**Locate favourite channels on eir TV**

There are 2 ways to locate your favourite channels list

**eirTV menu:**

- Go to the channels menu
- Select my favourite channels

**On live TV:**

- Click the up button
- Select favourite channel

**Controls**

- The controls are at the top of the app.
- You can pause, rewind, fast forward and much more.
- You can even swipe left or right to change channels.

**Change your streaming quality**

- You can choose between high and low streaming quality.
- Select the setting option on your mobile app.
- Select WiFi connection.
- Select Default WiFi bitrate option.
- The default mobile bitrate option to modify the streaming quality on Mobile Data.
- WiFi streaming is set to High by default, while mobile streaming is set to Low.

**Picture in Picture (PiP) - Android only**

This feature allows you to continue watching a programme in the eir TV app while using other apps on your device.

When you're watching Live TV in the eir TV app, you can exit the app and the video will continue playing in a small window in the bottom right-hand corner

- This screen can be moved around by dragging the window
- To return to full screen, just double-tap the app
- This feature can be enabled or disabled in the settings menu using the toggle options

**Managing eir TV app on devices**

eir TV app can only be registered on 2 alternative devices.

To remove or swap a device, please follow these steps:

- Log into [Login to the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "Login to the eir app ")(it does not have to be on the device you are trying to register).
- Click on 'more' on the top menu and select 'Manage eir TV' or 'eir TV and Amazon Prime Video'.
- Click on 'Remove this device' beside the device you are removing.
- Once the old device is removed, the new device should automatically register on the next login attempt.

There will be a notice above the devices advising you if you have a swap available or when you will next be able to swap a device. You can remove a device any time you wish, but you will not be able to register a new device unless you have a swap available or until the date listed on the eir app **.** You can swap a registered device once per calendar month.
