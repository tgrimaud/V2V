# 5G

eir has 99% 5G population coverage. We currently offer 5G coverage in all 26 counties of the Republic of Ireland.

**What's in it for the customer?**

- Get faster speed than ever before - 5G offers speeds up to 10 times faster than 4G.
- 5G means much less buffering, giving you seamless video calling and even smoother gaming.
- 5G provides a more reliable connection, with extra bandwidth to accommodate more traffic on the network.
- We have amazing 5G-enabled plans with No Limits Data, Unlimited Calls & texts and a huge selection of 5G phones!
- WiFi Calling.
- All our in market post pay and prepay plans include 5G data at no extra cost (excluding prepay Unlimited calls only plan per 14 days).

**What happens if I'm not in a 5G area?**

- If you are not in a 5G area, you will be automatically reverted to 4G. 5G will appear on your handset when you are in a 5G enabled area only. Remember you will need a 5G handset to be on the 5G network.
- While you may not live in a 5G area now, we are working to expand our 5G network and this may become available to you in the near future.
