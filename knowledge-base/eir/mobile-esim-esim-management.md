# eSIM management

**Moving from physical SIM card to eSIM**

If you have a physical SIM card in your phone you can easily move to an eSIM if your phone is eSIM compatible.

First, you will need to [request a SIM swap via the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "request a SIM swap via the eir app"), or by going into an eir retail store.

Once your SIM swap is complete you will recieve an email on how to set up your eSIM using a Qr Code.

You can also follow the steps in " [How do I activate my eSIM](https://eir.ie/helpandsupport/mobile/esim/esim-activation/ "How do I activate my eSIM")" to complete the activation.

Make sure to remove your physical SIM once your eSIM is up and running.

**I deleted my eSIM in error**

Don't worry if you delete you eSIM in error, you can log into the eir app to scan the QR code and activate the eSIM on your phone again.

Follow the steps outlined in " [How do I activate my eSIM](https://eir.ie/helpandsupport/mobile/esim/esim-activation/ "How do I activate my eSIM") " to complete the activation.

**I lost my phone with my eSIM, what do I do?**

If your phone is lost or stolen, [you can log into the eir app and complete a SIM replacement](https://my.eir.ie/login "you can log into the eir app and complete a SIM replacement") from the “Manage SIM & eSIM” page. This will allow you to request another eSIM or physical SIM replacement.

Alternatively you can go into any eir store to deactivate your eSIM and request a replacement SIM.

**Will my eSIM work while I'm roaming?**

Yes, your eSIM works in the very same way as a physical SIM when roaming. Just remember you need to turn on roaming for your eSIM in your phone settings.

**eSIM phone transfer**

Device transfer using your smartphone OS is currently not available, however you can follow these steps below to move your eSIM from one device to another.

1. Delete the eSIM off the phone that you no longer want to use the eSIM on.
2. [Log in to the eir app.](https://my.eir.ie/login "Log in to the eir app.")
3. Go to the “Manage SIM & eSIM” page.
4. Click on "QR code & passcode"to view your eSIM details.
5. Scan the QR code on the new phone to activate the eSIM.
6. Enter the passcode which will be displayed on screen.

**Please note**: In order for this to work, the eSIM must be deleted off of the original phone. If the eSIM was originally set-up via carrier activation you will not need to enter a passcode when activating your eSIM on your new device.
