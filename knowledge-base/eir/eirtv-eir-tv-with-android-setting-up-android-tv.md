# Setting up Android TV

**What arrives at your door?**

- Android Set Top Box
- Remote control
- Power supply
- AAA batteries

**Connecting the Android TV box**

To connect the Android TV box, please follow the steps below:

1\. Connect the set-top box to the TV using the supplied HDMI cable.

2\. Connect the power adapter to the set-top-box and plug it in a power socket.

3\. Make sure the TV is turned on.

4\. Select the TV input to which the set-top box is connected to.

5\. Once the connection is successfully established, the Welcome Assistant will appear on the TV screen.

**Initial set-up**

Following the steps below to set-up your new Android TV box.

1\. Plug it in and turn on your TV.

- Plug your Android TV into power and connect it to your TV with a HDMI cable.
- Turn on your TV and select the HDMI input that your Android TV is connected to.
- If you don't want to connect to Wi-Fi during set-up, you can connect your Android TV box to your router with an Ethernet cable.

2\. Choose your language.

3\. Choose if you would like to sync up your Android TV with your Android phone. You can skip this part if you want to just sign up with your Google account, or would rather not sign up with any account.

4\. Select your WiFi network if you are not using an ethernet cable.

5\. If you have not signed in with your Android phone you can now sign in with your Google account. This is recommended for the optimum viewing experience.

6\. Choose what apps you would like preloaded onto your Android TV box.

7\. When the set-up is complete you will taken straight into the eir TV interface.

**Do I need to have a Google account to access eir TV and additional apps with eir TV?**

No, eir TV will be available on your Android TV box when you complete your set-up along with a selection of preloaded apps.

However, if you want to donwload additional apps through the Google Play Store you will need to link your eir TV with your Google account. This can be done during the set up journey, or through the settings.

If you do not want to add your Google account to access additional apps you can skip this set up.

**Adding a Google accout to download additional apps**

If you have not added your Google account during the initial set-up journey you can add it by simply going to settings and following these steps.

1\. Select Accounts.

2\. Select sign in.

3\. Enter the email address which is attached to your Google account.

4\. Enter your password.

5\. You will then be prompted to confirm your login via your mobile phone.
