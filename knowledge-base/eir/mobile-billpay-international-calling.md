# International calling

**Making an international call**

When dialling a country outside of Ireland you will need to use the international dialling code of the country, while dropping the first zero of the number you are dialling.

**Please note:** There are certain countries that eir does not have international calling agreements with. If you are having issues when attempting to contact certain countries and the below troubleshooting has been carried out please contact customer care on **1901** to have the region you are contacting checked for such an agreement.

If you having problems calling an international country, please check the following things:

- Pay As You Go customers - have you enough credit to make the call?
- Bill Pay – are your account payments up to date?
- Are you using the international prefix before dialling the number?

If you’re still having problems try these troubleshooting tips:

- Try switching your phone off and on again or put your phone into airplane mode for at least 30 seconds, then switch it back to general. This will perform what's known as a 'location update' - giving a fresh connection to the network.
- Check your coverage - a network outage will always affect more than one person. You'll know there's an outage if other customers are having the same issue in your area.

**Why can't I make an international call?**

If you are having trouble making an international call, please first check:

- Have you paid your bill?
- Are you using the international prefix before dialling the number?

There are certain countries that eir does not have international calling agreements with. If you are having issues when attempting to contact certain countries and the below troubleshooting has been carried out please contact customer care on **1901** to have the region you are contacting checked for such an agreement.

**How do I bar international calls on my account?**

To bar international calling from your mobile text " **Intl No**" to **50046**.

It can take up to 24 hours for this change to occur.
