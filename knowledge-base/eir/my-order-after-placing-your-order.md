# After placing your order

**Your order confirmation**

Upon placing your order you will receive either a welcome email or a welcome letter depending on the contact information you provided to us when placing your order.

This confirmation will contain;

- Your eir account number.
- Your price incl. any activation fees
- Your contract length

**The communications we send you**

- Shortly after you sign up for an eir service, you will receive your Welcome Email or Letter.
- We will also send you confirmation of your direct debit details by post.
- You will also receive an appointment confirmation text message upon placing your order.
- Closer to the time of your appointment we will also send you a further reminder text message.

**Tracking your broadband or TV order**

- Once your order is fully processed, your modem or TV equipment should be delivered to you within 2-3 working days.
- Our courier company partner DPD will send you notifications with tracking numbers and updates by text message relating to your order. **Please note** neither eir or DPD will ask for any payment information in these text messages.
- If your broadband requires a technician installation the field technician will bring your modem equipment with them.
- Your TV order will always be delivered by our courier partner DPD.

**Tracking your mobile order**

- Once your order is fully processed, your mobile handset and or SIM card should be delivered to you within 2-5 working days.
- Our courier company partner DPD will send you notifications with tracking numbers and updates by text message relating to your order. **Please note** neither eir or DPD will ask for any payment information in these text messages.
- **For SIMs:** These are delivered by standard post within 1-3 working days.
- **For Handsets:** Allow 2-5 working days for delivery. Should any issue arise with your handset order, our Mobile fulfillment team will proactively contact you to advise the next steps.

**Changing your appointment date**

You can reschedule your appointment if you have received a text message from eir by [completing the change your appointment form.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=change-of-appointment "completing the change your appointment form.")
