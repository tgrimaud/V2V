# eir WiFi calling

**What is WiFi calling?**

eir Wifi calling is a free supplementary mobile service. This service allows you to call or text in an area that is not covered by mobile network signal. By connecting to any WiFi connection, you can now get even better mobile coverage anywhere in Ireland.

Better still you don't need any additional app for this service.

New and existing eir customers on an eligible eir Mobile plan with an enabled phone can avail of eir WiFi Calling.

[View our mobile plans](https://www.eir.ie/shop/mobile/bill-pay/ "View our mobile plans ") which include WiFi Calling.

**Benefits of WiFi calling**

- WiFi Calling allows you to use your mobile phone through any WiFi connection.
- Make calls and send texts anywhere in the world for same price you pay at home.
- You don't need any additional apps for this new service.
- All on your existing handset at no extra charge.
- Improves voice quality in areas where network coverage is weak.

This is ideal for customers who are in a poor indoor coverage area and have fixed services in the home.
