# Amazon Prime Video

**What is Amazon Prime Video?**

Amazon Prime Video allows you to watch or download Popular movies, TV series and award-winning Amazon Originals.

You also get:

- Ad-free uninterrrupted viewing
- Multiple users on up to three screens at once
- Instantly watch or download for offline viewing. Just select any titles available for download
- Access actor biographies, soundtracks, trivia and much more with X-Ray
- Watch on a variety of devices
- 4K UHD

**Who is eligible for Amazon Prime Video**

All residential eir TV customers are eligible for Amazon Prime Video.

Amazon Prime Video is not available for Business customers at this time.

**How to avail of Amazon Prime Video**

If you wish to avail of Amazon Prime Video, please follow the steps below:

- Go to the Amazon Prime Video banner and click on Get it Now.
- Register with Amazon: Existing Prime customers can Sign in but new customers must create an account.

Once the offer has been activated, you must download the app and sign in. It couldn't be easier, just follow these steps:

- Open the app store
- Search for the Prime Video app
- Tap to install for free
- Follow the on-screen instructions
- Sign-in to the app to immediately start enjoying Prime Video

**Cancel Amazon Prime Video**

Cancelling your Amazon Prime Video service can be done on your my eir account.

1. Login to your my eir account and Click eir TV and Amazon Prime Video
2. Click the Cancel Amazon Prime Video button
3. There will be a pop-up asking you to confirm the cancellation of your Amazon Prime Video service. Click Continue to Cancel.
