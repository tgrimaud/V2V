# How to set up Amazon Prime

**What do I need to access Amazon Prime with my eir account?**

All residential eir TV customers are eligible for Amazon Prime.

[You can sign up Amazon Prime via the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "You can sign up Amazon Prime via the eir app").

- New eir TV customers can start streaming within 30 minutes of the order being placed
- Existing eir TV customers just need to log in to start streaming.

**How do I add Amazon Prime via my eir account?**

If you wish to avail of Amazon Prime Video, please follow the steps below:

- Navigate to the Amazon Prime on your services card.
- Click on it and following the steps.
- Register with Amazon: Existing Prime customers can sign in but new customers must create an account.

Once the offer has been activated, you will have to download the Amazon Prime app and sign in. Then you can start streaming! It couldn't be easier, just follow these steps:

- Open the app store
- Search for the Prime Video app
- Tap to install for free
- Follow the on-screen instructions
- Sign-in to the app to immediately start enjoying Prime Video

**Cancel Amazon Prime.**

Cancelling your Amazon Prime Video service can be done via **the eir app.**

1. [Login to the eir app.](https://my.eir.ie/ "Login to the eir app.")
2. Click eir TV and Amazon Prime.
3. Select the Cancel Amazon Prime button.
4. There will be a pop-up asking you to confirm the cancellation of your Amazon Prime service.
5. Select Continue to Cancel.

**When will cancellation occur?**

If you decide to cancel Amazon Prime this will take effect from the end of your billing period.

Any adjustments to this add on price will reflect on the following bill.
