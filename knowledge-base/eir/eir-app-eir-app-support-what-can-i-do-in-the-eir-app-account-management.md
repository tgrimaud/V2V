# Account management

**View contract & account information.**

Managing your eir account has never been easier using the eir app. If you are trying to find out about your eir services, billing information about your account or your contract end date, please follow the below steps:

**Contract details:**

- Open the eir app and login.
- Navigate to the menu button in the top right-hand corner.
- Select 'Contract' to see the status of your current services with eir.

**Account details:**

- Open the eir app and login.
- Navigate to the menu button in the top right-hand corner.
- Select 'Account details' to view your login email address, payment method, billing address, primary contact number and any authorised users on your account.

**I have forgotten my password**

If you have forgotten your password, please follow the steps below to reset it:

1. Open the eir app and anter your email address and click 'Submit'.
2. Select 'Forgotten your password?'
3. Click I've forgotten my password; an email will be sent to your email address with details on resetting your password.

**Please note:** The link in the email will only be valid for 24 hours.

**How do I change my password?**

If you need to change your password, please follow the steps below:

1. Open the eir app and login with you existing credentials.
2. Navigate to the menu button in the top right-hand corner.
3. Click 'Account details'
4. Select 'Change' beside Password; an email will be sent to your email address with details on how to change your password.

**Please note:** The link in the email will only be valid for 24 hours.
