### F2000 speed

**Broadband speed check**

Visit our [broadband](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/broadbandspeedtest/) [sp](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/broadbandspeedtest/) [eed](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/broadbandspeedtest/) [checker](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/broadbandspeedtest/) to test your speed.

**What can affect my speed?**

The broadband speed you receive is influenced by several factors:

- The type of broadband service you have.
- The closer you are to the cabinet or exchange, the the better the speed.
- The type of internal network you have; while WiFi is convienient, you will generally get quicker and more consistent speeds on a wired network.
- Third party equipment can also have an effect on the speeds you receive; please refer to the manufacturers documentation to see if there are any speed constraints.

**What speed can I expect?**

There are two main elements to the speed you will experience in your home:

1. The speed coming down the cable into your house from outside (your line speed), we call this ‘line speed’ and is determined by your broadband type and how far you are from the local telephone exchange.
2. The wireless/WiFi signal within your house. [To get a better idea of the best options for your WiFi set up see our helpful guide.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/broadband/f2000/wifi/optimising-wifi-location/ "To get a better idea of the best options for your WiFi set up see our helpful guide.")

**Broadband speeds explained**

Broadband speed is measured in Mb/s (megabits per second):

- Download refers to the speed with which you can receive content from the Internet. This means how quickly you can download a game or a movie.

- Upload refers to the speed with which you can send content. This could be sending an email, uploading photos to a website or filling out a web order form.
