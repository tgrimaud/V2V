# eir Vision user guide

**How do I turn on / off my eir Vision+ box?**

- Press the power button in the top right-hand corner of your eir Vision remote to turn it on or off.
- The red standby light will change to white when powered on.
- The light on multiroom boxes will turn green.

**How do I find a programme I want to watch?**

The programme guide informs you what’s on all eir vision channels.

There are three ways to access the programme guide:

- Press **GUIDE** to open the full 7 day programme guide

While watching live TV

- Press the **left** and **right arrows** to bring up the mini programme guide
- Press the **up** and **down arrows** to bring up the channel list while continuing to watch live TV

**How do I record something on later?**

- Press **Guide** or select **programme guide** in the Menu
- Navigate to the programme you wish to record and press **REC** on your remote
- **REC** on your remote

**How do I modify a recording?**

If there is a clash of programmes you wish to record or if a live event goes on longer than scheduled, you may wish to modify your recordings

- Press **REC** to bring up the schedule recording pop-up
- Use the **left** and **right** **arrows** to modify the time
- Use the **up** and **down arrows** switch between the start and end time
- Press **OK** to confirm
- Cancel the selection by pressing **Back**

**How do I view a programme I have recorded?**

Recordings on your eir vision box are stored in **My Recordings**.

Within **My Recordings**, your recordings are stored in two places

- Series Recordings
- Recordings

**Series Recordings**

- Series recordings contain a folder for each series that you’ve recorded using the green button on your remote.
- Click on a folder to see all of the episodes recorded for that series.
- If you don’t see your recording in Series Recordings don’t worry, it’s most probably because your recording was a once off recording that’s stored in Recordings.

**Recordings**

Recordings contain any recording you’ve created on a once off basis using the **Rec (Record)** button on your remote.

**How do I pause live TV?**

- Simply press the play / pause button on your remote.
- Press it again when you are ready to continue watching.
- A small panel will appear showing the playback information; tThis will tell you how far behind the live broadcast you are.

**Please note:** This feature is not supported on the eir vision multiroom box.

**How do I rewind live TV?**

If you want to go back to see something again, press Rewind.

You can rewind back to the point at which you started watching the programme.

- Press Play/Pause and when you’re ready to continue viewing
- Press Fast-forward to skip ahead in a programme you've paused.

- Fast forwarding will automatically stop when it catches up with the live broadcast.
- A small panel will appear showing the playback information.
- This will tell you how far behind the live broadcast you are.

**How to set up your favourite channels**

- Go to the menu button on your remote control and scroll across to settings and select the channel management tab.
- Select **edit your favourite channels**

From the list of channels presented

- Scroll down and select the required channe
- Click the **OK** button on your remote to add the channel to your favourites list.
- When added to favourites a heart icon will appear beside the channel.
- Click the **OK** button again to remove the channel from favourites.

- Click the "OK" button on your remote to add the channel to your favourites list.
- When added to favourites a heart icon will appear beside the channel.
- Click the "OK" button again to remove  the channel from favourites
