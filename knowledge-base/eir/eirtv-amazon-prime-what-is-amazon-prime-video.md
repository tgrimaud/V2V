### What is Amazon Prime Video?

**What is Amazon Prime Video?**

Amazon Prime Video allows you to watch or download Popular movies, TV series and award-winning Amazon Originals.

You also get:

- Ad-free uninterrrupted viewing
- Multiple users on up to three screens at once
- Instantly watch or download for offline viewing. Just select any titles available for download
- Access actor biographies, soundtracks, trivia and much more with X-Ray
- Watch on a variety of devices
- 4K UHD

**Who is eligible for Amazon Prime?**

All residential eir TV customers are eligible for Amazon Prime, which includes Amazon Prime Video.

**How do I get support for Amazon Prime Video?**

If you require support on Prime Video, [you can visit the Prime Video help page.](https://www.primevideo.com/help/ref=atv_hp_nd_cnt?nodeId=202095490 "you can visit the Prime Video help page")
