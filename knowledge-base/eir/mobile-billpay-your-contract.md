# Your contract

**Upgrading**

If you're nearing the end of your current contract, you may be due an upgrade.

You can check if you’re eligible for an upgrade by:

- Visiting [the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "the eir app").
- Contact customer care on **1901**.
- Calling into one of our [retail stores](https://www.eir.ie/stores/ "retail stores").

You'll need to bring the following with you:

- Photo ID - must include date of birth and be in date/valid.

**Fair usage policy (FUP)**

For customers signing up from 22nd October 2024, the fair usage data limits for voice and 5G data do not apply on eir mobile connect plus 5G, eir mobile complete, eir mobile connect 30 day and eir mobile complete 30 day.

eir Mobile retains the right to take appropriate steps for traffic management, where for example other users are likely to be adversely affected by a Customer’s actions.

For customers that signed up before 22nd October 2024, eir is not enforcing data or voice fair usage caps on these plans either.

Text allowances where applicable in the Plans are subject to a fair usage policy of 10,000 texts.

Texts included in your mobile plan, used within Republic of Ireland and EU countries while roaming, are subject to fair usage.

The fair usage threshold in relation to data usage on the eir Mobile Essential Plan is 20GB.

Standard call, text and data rates apply to usage in excess of these limits, see our pricing page for a [see our pricing page for a full list of charges](https://www.eir.ie/group/pricing/eir_mobile/ "see our pricing page for a full list of charges").
