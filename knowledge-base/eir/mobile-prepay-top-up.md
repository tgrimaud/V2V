# Top-up

**How to top-up**

**It is important to make sure that you have the correct amount of credit in your account before opting-in to a bundle. See below to view our various top-up methods that are available:**

**Quick top-up**

[Top-up](https://www.eir.ie/mobile/top-up/quick-top-up/ "Top up") your eir mobile handset or the handset of another eir mobile customer.

**From your bank account**

You can top-up with selected banks online or their banking app. Check with your bank for further details.

**Vouchers**

Buy a voucher in any of our stores or in retailers nationwide and top up by dialling **1741** or texting the voucher code to **50103**.

**Scheduled top-up**

Make renewing your prepay offer easy by scheduling an automatic top up from your payment card a day before your offer is due to expire. Freephone **1740** or visit [the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/ "the eir app") to schedule your automatic top up.

**By phone**

It's simple to top up by phone. Just phone **1740** and have your debit or credit card ready.

**eir app**

You can top up via the **[eir app](https://my.eir.ie/ "eir app")** using a credit or debit card

**Useful tips**

Check your credit balance – Dial **\*#100#**

Check the days left on your offer – Text " **offer**" to **50104**

Check your data balance – Text " **balance data**" to **50104**

**How to check my balance**

Check your credit balance – Dial **\*#100#**

Check the days left on your offer – Text " **offer**" to **50104**

Check your data balance – Text **"balance data**" to **50104**

**My top-up hasn't been applied**

There could be several reasons why your top-up has not been applied.

- It can take to 24 hours for your payment to be processed and your selected plan to be activated.
- If you have opted into a prepay Simplicity plan, your credit will be taken to allow this plan to go active.
- If you find that after 24 hours, your plan is not active or you have not received any confirmation text, we recommend you check your method of payment. For example, if you have paid through your online banking, please ensure that your payment has been taken.
