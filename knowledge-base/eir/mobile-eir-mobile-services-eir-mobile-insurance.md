# eir mobile insurance

**Which devices can be covered by eir mobile insurance?**

eir mobile insurance is for smartphones purchased as new from eir at a price of up to €2,500 (including VAT).

**What is covered by eir mobile insurance?**

Your policy covers your insured device during the period specified in your Certificate of Insurance for:

- Accidental damage and liquid damage to the insured device
- Theft of the insured device

For more information about your insurance cover [read the full terms and conditions.](https://eir.ie/.content/pdf/terms/eirmobile/eir-Mobile-Insurance-Terms-and-Conditions-From-September-2026.pdf "read the full terms and conditions."). [See here for product information.](https://eir.ie/.content/pdf/terms/eirmobile/eir-Mobile-Insurance-IPID-From-September-2026.pdf "See here for product information.")

**Can I cancel eir mobile insurance?**

You may withdraw from your policy within the 14 working days of the cooling of period after the start date, and without giving any reason.

You may also cancel your policy any time after the 14 working days cooling of period, with no less than 30 consecutive days written notice.

[Read the full terms and conditions of eir mobile insurance](https://eir.ie/.content/pdf/terms/eirmobile/eir-Mobile-Insurance-Terms-and-Conditions-From-September-2026.pdf "Read the full terms and conditions of eir mobile insurance") to learn more about contract withdrawal and cancellation.

**What is the difference between warranty and eir mobile insurance?**

Warranty covers damage that involves a lack of function that has arisen from a manufacturing defect (mechanical and electrical failures). That is, the mobile device has stopped working without external influences.

eir Mobile Insurance covers unexpected events that result from an accident such as cracks in the screen, as determined in the policy terms and conditions.

You are allowed to make up to two claims per year.

All repairs are carried out in authorized repair stores using original parts. The warranty remains even after a repair, as the warranty is provided by manufacturer and the insurance is provided by the insurer.

**How many claims does eir mobile insurance cover?**

You are permitted to make a maximum of 2 (two) claims during each and every 12 (twelve) month period of cover, which begins on the effective date, which is the date your insurance cover becomes effective as shown on your Certificate of Insurance.  Only 1 (one) replacement of the Insured Device is permitted in any 12 (twelve) month period.

**What do I need to do to submit a claim?**

The easiest way to create a claim is to log in to the customer portal: [https://eirmobileinsurance.bolttech.ie](https://eirmobileinsurance.bolttech.ie/)

In order for us to be able to help you faster, we ask you to have your insurance policy and purchase receipt on hand when you contact us.

Depending on the nature of the claim, you might need to submit more documentation, such as a Garda Report for Theft claims.
