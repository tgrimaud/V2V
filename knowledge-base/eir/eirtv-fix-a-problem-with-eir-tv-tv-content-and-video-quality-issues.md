Cables and connections

If the Apple TV and the television volume are working but there is still no sound, checking the cables is the next step

- Locate the HDMI cable and unplug it at both ends, then plug it back in
- Try swapping the ends of the HDMI. In other words, plug the end that was in the television into the Apple TV box and vice versa
- If you have a different HDMI cable, try using that one. Also try using a different HDMI port on your TV
- Try a different device in each of the HDMI ports
