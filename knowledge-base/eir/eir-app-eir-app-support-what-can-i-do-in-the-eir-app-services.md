# Services

**Sign up for Amazon Prime.**

You can sign up for Amazon Prime through the eir app by following the steps below:

1. Open the eir app and log in.
2. Select 'Services' in the bottom menu.
3. Under the TV tab, select 'Sign up for Amazon Prime'.
4. A new window will appear, click 'Get Amazon Prime'.
5. You must agree to the terms and conditions' before selecting 'Register now'
6. Once the offer has been activated, you must download the app and sign in.

**Manage your registered devices.**

With the eir app you can manage you eir TV service by updating your registered devices. To update your registered devices follow the steps below:

1. Open the eir app and log in.
2. Select 'Services' in the bottom menu.
3. Under the TV tab, select 'Manage your devices'
4. Any active devices will be displayed.
5. Only two devices can be registered at once, but you can swap a registered device once per calendar month.
6. To change a device select 'Delete' beside the device you wish to remove.

**Please note:** The date on which you will be able to change a registered device will be displayed above the devices.

**Manage eir TV PIN.**

With the eir app you can manage you eir TV service by updating your PIN following the steps below:

1. Open the eir app and log in.
2. Select 'Services' in the bottom menu.
3. Under the TV tab, select 'Manage my PIN'
4. Your existing PIN will display along with the option to enter a new PIN.
5. Enter a new PIN and click 'Save'.

**Activate eSIM**

To activate your eSIM using QR code, follow the steps below:

1. [Login to the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "Login to the eir app") on a laptop, tablet or another device. Go to your eir account to activate your eSIM. If you are not already registered for the eir app you will need to do this first.
2. Click on 'Activate eSIM': There will be an option to activate your eSIM at the top of your dashboard or under My Services.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone.
4. Lastly, enter the passcode. Once the eSIM activation is in progress, you will be asked for a passcode. Enter the passcode displayed within the pop up on screen.

**Replacment SIM.**

A replacement SIM card can be ordered through the eir app by following the steps below:

1. Open the eir app and login.
2. Navigate to the menu button in the top right-hand corner.
3. Select 'SIM replacement'.
4. Click 'Order'.

**View your mobile PIN/PUK.**

Your PIN and PUK are available to view on the eir app for all eir mobile customers. To view these details, please follow the steps below:

1. Open the eir app and log in.
2. Select 'Services' in the bottom menu.
3. Under the Mobile, select 'PIN & PUK'
4. Your PIN and PUK will be displayed.

If you are trying to retrieve a Network Unlock code, you can complete our [Network Unlock code form](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/forms/?form=unlock-a-mobile).
