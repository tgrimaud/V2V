# Remotes - eir TV and Siri

**Get to know your eir TV remote**

- To move around the screen you can press left, right, up, or down to navigate the menus.
- To select an item press the centre button.

If you would like to find out more about your eir Remotes, [please see our helpful guide.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/#/device/eir/tv/topic/setup/overview-of-the-remote-buttons/1 "please see our helpful guide.")

**Siri remote**

- To move around the screen, you can swipe up, down, left, or right
- To select an item, highlight it then press to click the Touch surface
- To scroll through lists, quickly swipe up or down multiple times
- If an index is next to the list, swipe right. Highlight a letter to go to that place in the list

**Menu:** Press one time to return to the previous screen. Press and hold to go to the Home screen

Apple TV App/Home: To see your recently used apps, double press Apple TV App/Home.

To open the app press Apple TV App/Home and again to go to the Home screen.

**eir Traditional Remote - Setup instructions**

1. First, remove the plastic protection around the batteries on the remote
2. If you have a Samsung TV, the remote should already work. If not, follow the instructions below
3. Hold down both the menu and OK buttons together, until the red LED above the menu button turns on
4. Now point the remote at your TV and press the P^ button, until the volume menu appears on your TV screen - Check the VOL+ and VOL- buttons work to control the volume on your TV.
5. If they do, press the OK button and setup is complete. If they do not work, go back to step 3 above.
