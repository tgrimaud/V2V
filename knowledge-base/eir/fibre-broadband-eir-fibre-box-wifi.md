### eir fibre box WiFi

**Reset WiFi settings**

**How do I restore the modem's WiFi settings?**

To revert to your modem's WiFi default Network Name and Password, you must restore the modem's factory settings.

You will need to press and hold the reset button, which is located on the back of the modem, for 8-10 seconds.
