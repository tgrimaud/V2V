# eSIM activation

**How do I activate my eSIM with QR code?**

To activate your eSIM using QR code, follow the steps below:

1. [Login to the eir app](https://my.eir.ie/login "Login to the eir app") on a laptop, tablet or another device. Go to your eir account to activate your eSIM. If you are not already registered for the eir app you will need to do this first.
2. Click on 'Activate eSIM': There will be an option to activate your eSIM at the top of your dashboard or under My Services.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone.
4. Lastly, enter the passcode. Once the eSIM activation is in progress, you will be asked for a passcode. Enter the passcode displayed within the pop up on screen.

**How do I activate my eSIM through carrier activation through EID?**

For a seamless eSIM set up, some brands have an automated process. Providing your EID number ensures the quickest set up of your eSIM. To activate eSIM through carrier activation, please follow the steps below.

1. Enter your EID from phone settings when ordering an eSIM sim only plan with eir mobile.
2. A digital eSIM profile linked to your EID will be sent securely to your phone.
3. You’ll receive a push notification to your phone, follow the on-screen instructions to complete your eSIM set-up

**Please note:** If you purchased a phone as part of your order, your eSIM will be ready to activate when you turn on your new phone.

**Having trouble activating my eSIM?**

If you are having trouble activating your eSIM follow the steps below:

1. Restart your device: A simple reboot can resolve minor software glitches affecting your eSIM.
2. Toggle airplane mode: Turn airplane mode on for a few seconds and then off to refresh your connection.
3. Check your eSIM selection: Go to your phone's mobile/connection settings and confirm the correct eSIM profile is selected for mobile data, calls, and texts.

If you are still having issues with your eSIM go into any eir store or contact customer care.

**Does my phone need to be unlocked?**

Yes, if you have a SIM only plan your phone must be unlocked in order for you to activate your eir eSIM.

To check if your phone is unlocked:

- For iPhone: Settings > General > About > Carrier Lock: If it says 'No SIM restrictions' your phone is unlocked.
- For Android: Settings > Connections > Mobile Networs > Network Operators > Turn off 'Select automatically'. If your only  your current network is listed you phone is likely to be locked.

If your phone is locked, you will need to contact your current provider and request to have it unlocked.
