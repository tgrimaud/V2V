# Mobile broadband

**How to set up my mobile broadband**

- Ensure your mobile broadband device is powered on.
- On your WiFi enabled device (i.e. your mobile phone, laptop, or tablet) go to the wireless connection settings.
- Select your hotspot (WiFi Name) from the list of available connections.
- The Wifi name and WiFi password will be on a label on the bottom of the router.
- You will be asked to enter the WiFi password - input the WiFi security key (this is an 8 digit password)
- Press OK.

**How do I set up a WiFi hotspot?**

- On your WiFi enabled device (i.e. your mobile phone, laptop, computer or tablet) go to the wireless connection settings and select your hotspot from the list of available connections. The name (or SSID) of your hotspot will be written on a sticker on the back. Then input the WiFi security key (this is an 8 digit password) and press OK.
- Enter your PIN. You will need to input this via the 'web management' page on your WiFi enabled device. To get to this page, open a web browser, type **192.168.8.1** (or **192.168.1.1** if you are using one of our older devices) into the address bar and press enter.
- Enter your PIN. You'll find this on the back of your SIM pack.

**How do I update my APN settings?**

Should you need to enter your APN settings manually, please enter the details below:

- **Connection name:** eir Mobile data
- **Data bearer:** Packet data or GPRS on some handsets
- **Access Point Name (APN):** broadband.myeirmobile.ie
- **Username:** N/A
- **Prompt password:** N/A
- **Password:** N/A
- **Authentication:** None
- **Homepage:** [https://my.eir.ie/](https://my.eir.ie/ "my eir")

**Optimising your mobile broadband device**

- Remove any unnecessary connections from the device. The maximum device connection possible on the Huawei E5577 is 16.
- Know your limit. Check your data allowance on My eir to make sure to have not reached your limit during this billing cycle. Your allowance is 100GB per billing cycle.
- Move the E5577 router to a suitable location near the devices connected.

**Where can I find my eir mobile broadband number?**

You can find your eir mobile broadband number on the web based management page.

Simply go to Tools / Diagnostics, click on the 'SIM' tab, and then choose 'Own number' (MSISDN).

**Optimising your modem**

- Power off devices that are not in use to be able get the maximum speed possible.
- Place your device in a suitable location in the home or office where you tend to have the most devices connected.
- Switch to a wired connection via an Ethernet cable for a more steady connection.

**Parental controls**

- Encourage your child to always use child-friendly search engines, such as [Swiggle](http://www.swiggle.org.uk/ "Swiggle") or [Kids-search](http://www.kids-search.com/ "Kids-search").
- Safe search settings can be activated on [Google](http://www.google.com/familysafety "Google") and [Bing](http://www.bing.com/preferences.aspx "Bing").
- For other search engines go to safety settings.
- See here for how to turn the safety mode on [YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/174084?hl=en-GB "YouTube"), [iTunes](http://support.apple.com/kb/ht1904 "iTunes") and [Google Play](https://www.androidcentral.com/setting-kid-friendly-android-device "Google Play").
