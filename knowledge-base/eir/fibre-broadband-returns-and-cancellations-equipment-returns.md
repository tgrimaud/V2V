### Equipment returns

**Important information about eir equipment returns**

If you have recently cancelled some or all of your services with eir, we will send you a letter advising what equipment you will need to return.

Any equipment will need to be returned within 30 days of the cancellation date outlined on the letter.If equipment we request to be returned has not been returned you will be liable for a returned equipment fee.

**Please note:** Fibre to the Home customers should not return the optical fibre termination point (ONT) as it is used by other service providers.

You can use any of our options below to return your equipment to us.

You can return your equipment by: (Please note you must have received a return's letter first).

- Dropping to an eir retail store. [Visit our store page to find your nearest retail store](https://www.eir.ie/stores/ "Visit our store page to find your nearest retail store").
- Booking an eir courier to collect from an address of your choice.

If you would like to use one of the above return methods, you will need to log the collection. [To log the collection please use this form](http://eirreturns.blocworx.com/ "To log the collection please use this form").  You will need your account number.

**How can I change my eir courier booking?**

- If you need to make any changes to your booking please contact DPD on 09064 20500 and quote the reference number which can be found in the booking confirmation email.
- Or you can [contact DPD by email.](mailto:customerservices@dpd.ie "contact DPD by email") Please include your DPD tracking number.

**I’ve lost my label, what should I do?**

For fixed line equipment ( **modems, set top boxes, apple TV’s & home plugs**) [you can request a label here](https://eirreturns.blocworx.com/ "you can request a label here"). Simply enter your account number and book your return option.

**What is the proper way to package my items?**

Please ensure all items are packaged in a suitable sized box. If you cannot find a box that will contain all equipment together, please box each item individually. If you are returning your items through the eir Courier Collection and using multiple boxes there is no need to secure the boxes together.

However, if you are returning to a retail store, please secure the boxes together to ensure we receive all the equipment and you are not charged on your final bill.

**I cannot find information on equipment returns in the T&C’s of my contract?**

The information on equipment returns is in the [eir general terms and conditions](https://www.eir.ie/opencms/export/sites/default/.content/pdf/terms/general/eir_general_terms_and_conditions.pdf).
