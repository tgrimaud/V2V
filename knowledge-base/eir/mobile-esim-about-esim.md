# About eSIM

**How long will it take to receive an eSIM?**

If you've ordered an eSIM only, you'll recieve your eSIM instantly once your order is processed. An email will then be sent to you with a step by step guide on how to activate your eSIM. Simply follow the "How do I activate my eSIM" steps and you're good to go.

If you've ordered a phone to be delivered to you, once you receive your phone, turn it on and you will be ready to set up your eSIM. You will also get an email with a step by step guide on how to activate your eSIM.

Don't worry if your eSIM doesn't automatically download, [you can also activate your eSIM via QR code within the eir app.](https://my.eir.ie/login "you can also activate your eSIM via QR code within the eir app. ")

**How do I know if my phone is eSIM compatible?**

You need to see if your phone has an EID number as only eSIM compatible phones will have this unique number.

1. In your phone settings:
   - For **Android devices** \- naviagate to Settings > General > Status > SIM Status
   - For **iPhone** \- navigate to Settings > General > About and scroll down to find EID
2. Dial **\*#06#** on your device keypad to display the EID

**Can I have an eSIM and physical SIM on one phone?**

Yes, once your phone supports dual sim you can have an eSIM and a physical SIM. Many smartphones including both Android and iPhone have this functionality allowing you to use different phone numbers simultaneously.

**Wearable device eSIM compatability**

We do not currently support eSIM for wearable devices e.g. smart watches.

**How can I remove an eSIM?**

There is no physical SIM to remove but you can delete the eSIM profile from your device.

**Can I install more than one eSIM on my phone?**

Yes, once your device is compatible you can have multiple eSIM profiles on your device. However you can only have 2 SIMs active at any one time.

**Do I need to use a pin code with my eSIM?**

No, unlike physical SIM cards, eSIM doesn't require you to use a pin code. However, you can turn this on if you prefer.
