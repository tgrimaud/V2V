# TV parental controls

**What is parental control?**

Parental control will allow you to rate the content available on your eir vision box and block channels of your choice.

**How do I set it up parental control**

- Parental control is located in the settings menu on your eir vision box.
- Enter your Pin - by default the pin is set to 1111 (if you experience any issues with this please try, 0000, or 1234)

- Select parental control
- You will be presented with the available age restrictions for content viewing.
- Scroll to the required age selection and press the OK button to select
- Within settings scroll across to the parental control tab
- Enter your PIN - by default the pin is set to 1111 (if you experience any issues with this please try, 0000, or 1234)

- NB This will only restrict channels and programmes which broadcast an age guideline. Not all channels/programmes do this.

- To block channel access, in Parental Control settings select “Channel Blocking” to restrict access to the required channels.

**How to restrict TV channels**

- If you wish to change the Parental Rating you have set go to parental control and enter your PIN.
- You can then select a new age rating for your eir vision box.
- You can choose to set the age rating to G, PG, 12, 15 or 18.
- If you require any help, please call our Technical Support team on **1901**.

**Will I have parental controls in each room with TV?**

Yes, all eir vision multiroom boxes in your home will be controlled from the eir vision+ box parental control setting so whichever setting you have chosen will apply throughout your home.
