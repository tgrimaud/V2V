### eir WiFi 6 booster

**What is eir WiFi 6 booster?**

eir WiFi 6 booster is a device to extend your modem’s WiFi signal range.

Unlike homeplugs/WiFi extenders, it extends the WiFi signal without having to run this signal through the powercables in your home.

**Connecting to your eir fibre box**

1. Turn on the WiFi 6 booster by plugging it in and pressing the on/off button.
2. Connect the Ethernet cable provided, to the fibre box and WiFi booster.

The yellow connectors go into the yellow ports
3. Wait until all 3 LEDs are green on the WiFi booster.
4. The WiFi booster will adopt the WiFi name of your fibre box.
5. You can now move the WiFi booster to your desired location and it will connect WIRELESSLY to the fibre box.
6. The LINK LED will flash red while the WiFi booster pairs wirelessly to the fibre box, allow up to 15mins.
7. For best results position the WiFi booster halfway between the fibre box and your WiFi black spots. While trying to keep the LINK LED as close as possible to green.
8. Your devices will connect to the WiFi booster wirelessly automatically once they are connected to your fibre box. You can also connect up to two devices with a wired connection using the two Etherent (yellow) ports at the rear of the WiFi booster.

**Will the eir WiFi 6 booster work with my modem?**

Yes the eir WiFi 6 booster will work with all modems. If you have a modem other than an [eir fibre box](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/smartphonehelp/#/device/eir/fibre-box "eir fibre box ") or later, you will need a minimum of two devices and need to follow our [companion set-up.](https://www.eir.ie/helpandsupport/broadband/eir-fibre-box/wifi/smart-wifi/)

Should you wish to buy an F3000 device you can go to our [accessories website](https://eir-store.ie/collections/smart-wi-fi) or purchase by calling our Customer support teams.
