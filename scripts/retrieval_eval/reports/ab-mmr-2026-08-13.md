# MMR A/B — TASK-BE-028 (2026-08-13, live local backend)

Ran against the **live local backend** on `:8081` (same build, only the MMR config changed),
pgvector + Ollama `nomic-embed-text`, corpus loaded, `--top-k 8`, `--domain-mode none`,
14 questions / 29 variants. Per-arm JSON/MD: `baseline-mmr-off`, `baseline-mmr-on`,
`baseline-mmr-l09` (`2026-08-13`).

## Result

| Arm (fetch ×3) | recall@4 | recall@8 | MRR | phrasing-stability | guardrail-block | retrieval-eviction | new unstable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MMR off** (dense-only baseline) | 0.828 | **0.897** | 0.771 | **0.786** | 2 | 1 | — |
| MMR λ=0.7 (initial default) | 0.828 | 0.862 ⬇ | 0.760 | 0.714 ⬇ | 2 | 2 ⬇ | `sup-fr-tv` |
| MMR λ=0.9 | **0.862** | 0.897 | 0.772 | 0.786 | 2 | 1 | — |

## Reading

- **λ=0.7 is harmful.** recall@8 0.90→0.86, stability 0.79→0.71, and it introduced a *new*
  eviction (`sup-fr-tv`). Root cause: `nomic-embed-text` cosine scores are compressed
  (~0.49–0.80, no task prefix), so at λ=0.7 the `(1−λ)·redundancy` term (Jaccard 0–1) overwhelms
  the small relevance spread and displaces relevant chunks.
- **λ=0.9 is neutral.** Identical recall@8 / MRR / stability to the baseline, +0.03 recall@4
  (one variant), and it does remove the observed `305,305` near-duplicate crowding. No downside
  observed, but no gain on the acceptance bar either.
- **MMR does not fix the one eviction.** `sup-fr-slow` is evicted **only on the greeting-prefixed
  variant** "Bonjour, internet est très lent chez moi." — the acceptable answer chunk
  (`13`/`854`/`telecom-faq.md`) is **absent from the candidate set entirely** for that phrasing
  (the non-greeting variant hits it at rank 2). MMR can only reorder what is fetched, so a recall
  miss is out of its reach. This is a **BUG-003-class phrasing-stability flip**: the leading
  "Bonjour," pollutes the query embedding.

## Decision

- **Ship MMR disabled by default** (`voice-support.knowledge.retrieval.mmr.enabled=false`), keep
  it as a **tested, env-toggleable dedup guard** with a safe `lambda=0.9` preset. The
  eval-harness's whole purpose is to stop us shipping an ineffective lever on intuition — here the
  intuitive λ=0.7 was net-negative.
- **The real lever for the one eviction is recall, not diversity:** normalize the query before
  embedding (strip a leading greeting such as "Bonjour,") and/or add hybrid lexical (`tsvector`)
  fusion. Recommended as the OQ-008 next step (see ADR-0032).
- **The EN gap remains guardrail over-block** (BUG-009), unchanged by MMR and out of OQ-008 scope.

## Reproduce

```bash
# MMR off
KB_RETRIEVAL_MMR_ENABLED=false java -jar backend/target/voice-support-backend-*.jar --server.port=8081
python3 scripts/retrieval_eval/run_eval.py --base-url http://localhost:8081 --top-k 8 --label mmr-off-2026-08-13
# MMR on (sweep lambda)
KB_RETRIEVAL_MMR_ENABLED=true KB_RETRIEVAL_MMR_LAMBDA=0.9 java -jar ... --server.port=8081
python3 scripts/retrieval_eval/run_eval.py --base-url http://localhost:8081 --top-k 8 --label mmr-l09-2026-08-13
```
