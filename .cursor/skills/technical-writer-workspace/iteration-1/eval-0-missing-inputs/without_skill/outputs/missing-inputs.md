# Missing Galaxion / BSS Inputs Before Invoice Comparison

## Objective

This document lists the information the Voice Support Bot team needs from the
Galaxion BSS team before implementing invoice comparison.

The goal is to avoid building comparison logic against guessed data shapes. Each
input below should confirm how to retrieve invoices, how to reconcile invoice
amounts, and how to explain a delta to a customer without inventing missing
business evidence.

## Requested Delivery Package

Ask the BSS team for one small, anonymized package that can be shared with the
engineering team and used as the reference fixture set.

The package should include:

- two consecutive invoices for the same customer account;
- the PDF files exactly as generated by Galaxion;
- the BSS identifiers needed to retrieve those invoices again;
- the API responses used to find and download the invoice documents;
- the business events that explain the difference between the two invoices;
- the expected customer-facing explanation validated by billing stakeholders.

Prefer one simple account first. If possible, add a second account with a more
complex invoice delta, such as an expired discount, prorated subscription,
out-of-bundle usage, one-off fee, adjustment, or option activated mid-period.

## Priority 1 - Invoice Discovery And Download

Before implementation, confirm the exact Galaxion flow used to locate an invoice
for a customer.

Requested inputs:

- the service and endpoint used to list invoice periods for an account;
- the service and endpoint used to search invoice documents;
- the service and endpoint used to download the invoice PDF;
- the required query criteria for `bill-run-documents/search`;
- the identifiers returned by the search response;
- the identifier that must be passed to the document download endpoint;
- example responses for successful search, no result, and multiple results.

For each provided invoice, include:

- `accountId`;
- `customerId`, if distinct from the account identifier;
- `invoiceNumber`;
- `billPeriodId`;
- `billRunId`;
- `billRunAccountId`;
- document identifier;
- invoice date;
- billing period start and end dates;
- currency;
- invoice status.

Why this is needed:

- the bot must retrieve the correct current and previous invoice;
- the mock BSS must mirror the real lookup path;
- invoice comparison must be based on stable identifiers, not filenames or
  assumptions from the PDF layout.

## Priority 2 - Invoice PDF Structure

The comparison engine will not ask the LLM to calculate invoice amounts directly
from the PDF. The PDF must first be extracted into structured JSON and reconciled
against known totals.

Requested inputs:

- two or more anonymized invoice PDFs generated by the real Galaxion flow;
- a short description of every visible invoice section;
- examples of subscription charges, usage charges, discounts, taxes, one-off
  fees, adjustments, and previous balance lines when available;
- confirmation of which sections are customer-facing and which sections are
  internal or operational only;
- the expected extracted JSON for at least one invoice, if the BSS team already
  has a structured representation.

Why this is needed:

- the PDF extractor needs real layout examples;
- reconciliation rules must match the real invoice presentation;
- downstream explanations must reference fields that actually exist.

## Priority 3 - Amount Semantics And Reconciliation

Confirm how Galaxion represents money and how invoice totals should be
reconciled.

Requested inputs:

- whether API amounts are represented as cents, decimal major units, or strings;
- whether PDF amounts are tax-included, tax-excluded, or both;
- tax rounding rules;
- discount representation: negative line, section total, or separate adjustment;
- whether invoice total includes previous balance, payments, credit notes, or
  only current-period charges;
- how prorated charges are represented;
- how commercial gestures and adjustments are represented;
- expected total calculation for the provided sample invoices.

Why this is needed:

- the comparison must not subtract amounts with different meanings;
- the bot must explain deltas in business terms, not arithmetic artifacts;
- tests need a trusted expected total for every sample invoice.

## Priority 4 - Business Causes For Invoice Deltas

For each invoice delta scenario, provide the BSS evidence that proves the cause.

Requested scenarios:

- expired discount;
- new option or service activated during the billing period;
- cancelled option or service;
- offer change;
- prorated subscription;
- out-of-bundle voice, SMS, data, or roaming usage;
- adjustment or regularization;
- one-off fee;
- tax change;
- credit note or refund.

For each scenario, provide:

- the Galaxion service that owns the information;
- the endpoint or event source to query;
- the relevant fields and identifiers;
- the effective date;
- the amount impact;
- the invoice line or section where the impact appears;
- accepted customer-facing wording for the explanation.

Why this is needed:

- the comparison engine must connect amount differences to real business causes;
- the RAG/LLM layer must be grounded in evidence;
- unsupported scenarios can be escalated instead of guessed.

## Priority 5 - Customer And Account Identification

Confirm how the bot receives or resolves the customer context before it retrieves
invoice data.

Requested inputs:

- the customer identifier available in the voice or web journey;
- the mapping between customer, contact, account, contract, and subscription;
- the minimum identity verification level required before invoice exposure;
- whether multiple accounts can be linked to one customer;
- how to choose the target account when several are eligible;
- masking rules for invoice identifiers, amounts, addresses, and personal data;
- log redaction requirements.

Why this is needed:

- invoice data must not be exposed to the wrong customer;
- the BSS adapter needs the right lookup key;
- the support flow needs clear escalation rules when identity is incomplete.

## Priority 6 - Error Handling And Operational Constraints

Confirm the behavior the bot should implement when Galaxion cannot provide a
complete comparison.

Requested inputs:

- standard Galaxion error response format;
- authentication and authorization failure behavior;
- not-found behavior for customer, account, invoice, and document;
- behavior when several documents match the same criteria;
- timeout values and retry guidance;
- pagination limits;
- rate limits;
- expected behavior during partial BSS outage;
- whether stale or cached invoice data is allowed;
- escalation wording for unavailable invoice data.

Why this is needed:

- the bot must fail closed for sensitive customer data;
- operational errors should produce clear support guidance;
- the mock BSS must include realistic failure fixtures.

## Priority 7 - Access And Environments

Before coding against the real BSS, confirm the access model.

Requested inputs:

- target sandbox or integration environment;
- base URLs for required services;
- authentication mechanism;
- required scopes or roles;
- network allowlist or VPN requirements;
- Swagger/OpenAPI URLs for the selected endpoints;
- sample anonymized credentials or a secure onboarding path;
- contact person for contract questions and incident triage.

Why this is needed:

- engineering can validate the contract against a real environment;
- the mock can stay contract-compatible with Galaxion;
- implementation can avoid hardcoded environment assumptions.

## Minimum Inputs To Start Implementation

Implementation should not begin until these minimum inputs are available:

- two consecutive anonymized invoice PDFs for the same account;
- the identifiers and API responses used to retrieve those PDFs;
- confirmed amount semantics and expected totals;
- at least one validated invoice delta cause with BSS evidence;
- customer/account identification rules;
- error behavior for invoice not found and document not found.

If only these minimum inputs are available, the team can start with a narrow
comparison slice:

- retrieve two invoice documents from the mock BSS;
- extract invoice JSON from the PDFs;
- reconcile totals;
- compare sections and line groups;
- produce an evidence-based explanation for the validated delta scenario;
- escalate all unsupported causes.

## Open Questions For The BSS Team

- Is `billing-api` the only supported entry point for invoice documents in V1?
- Is there any structured invoice-line endpoint, or is PDF extraction the only
  available source for detailed lines?
- Which identifier is stable across billing periods: customer, account,
  contract, subscription, or another Galaxion-specific identifier?
- Can one account have several invoice documents for the same period?
- Are invoice PDFs regenerated after adjustments, or are adjustments emitted as
  separate documents?
- Are credit notes comparable with invoices, or should they follow a separate
  flow?
- Which business events are authoritative for discount expiration and option
  activation?
- Which invoice information may the bot speak aloud on a voice channel?
- Which missing-data cases must be escalated to a human advisor immediately?

## Expected Outcome

Once these inputs are provided, the team can define the `BssBillingPort`,
implement a contract-compatible mock, build deterministic invoice extraction
fixtures, and implement invoice comparison without depending on guessed Galaxion
behavior.
